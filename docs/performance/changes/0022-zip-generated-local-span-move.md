# ZIP generated local-span ownership move

Date: 2026-08-11

Production base: `b974fa4e45e66b604a62c0f9aa6dd14b0f2f7f16`

Scope: the shared ZIP preservation writer used by targeted OOXML/OPC
publication. OLE2, RTF and ODF production code are unchanged, and iWork/IWA
crates were explicitly excluded.

## Hypothesis

Change 0021 removed the 4 MiB logical Part handoff copy. Its after profile then
separated two similarly sized allocations inside
`soapberry_zip::preserve::generated_entry`:

1. Deflate grows the complete generated one-member archive buffer. That buffer
   is required so every generated entry and the complete preservation plan can
   be validated before a sequential sink receives bytes.
2. After validation, `bytes[..directory_offset].to_vec()` allocated and copied
   the complete 4.20 MiB local member solely to retain it in `PreparedEntry`.

The second allocation is unnecessary because the validated archive vector
already owns the exact local bytes.

## Change

`generated_entry` now scopes the same ZIP64/layout/entry validation over the
complete archive, then moves the validated backing vector into
`PreparedLocal::Generated`. `Vec::split_off` retains only the small central
directory and EOCD tail; truncation removes the EOCD from the stored central
record. No generated byte is exposed before validation finishes.

The Deflate buffer, complete preservation-plan preflight, raw local/central
framing, bounded copy and sink adapters, ZIP64/layout fallback, error ordering,
and partial-output accounting remain unchanged. No public API, dependency,
format facade, source authorization, executor, cache, lock, unsafe code,
resource limit, signature policy or failure-atomicity boundary changes.

## Small, edge and exact-no-op guardrails

The broad many-small process initially contained a noisy +29.5% compressible
p95 despite a -1.01% p50 and +3.17% mean. A dedicated run still showed strong
early/late host drift, so four repeated short ABBA cycles were used rather than
hiding that trigger. Pooling 8,000 samples per state gives:

| Many-small mutation | p50 delta | p95 delta | p99 delta | Mean delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Compressible | +0.90% | -2.41% | +1.60% | -0.04% |
| Incompressible | +0.81% | -0.26% | -22.03% | +0.09% |

A separate 20,000-sample-per-leg tiny matrix resolves its original relative
tail trigger: compressible/incompressible mean deltas are +0.69%/+0.74%, p50
+3.20%/+1.80%, and p95 -10.73%/-5.21%. The 2,048-Part wide-root p50/mean/p95
deltas are -1.88%/-1.72%/-0.52% for compressible input and
+0.40%/+0.68%/+2.69% for incompressible input.

Exact owned-source no-op publication cannot call `generated_entry`. Its
20,000-sample timer-floor guard is unchanged for tiny inputs. Wide-root
compressible p50/mean/p95 moves -1.29%/-3.13%/-12.43%; incompressible moves
+3.15%/+4.27%/+2.33%. All remain within the 5% review gate on p50 and mean.

Raw guard reports are the `abba-opc-local-span-move-{small,edge,tiny,noop,
noop-high}-*.json` and `abba-opc-local-span-repeat-*.json` files under
`results/`.

## Allocation, counters and RSS

Matched one-shot Heaptrack on the few-large incompressible case removes the
single 4.20 MiB `try_allocate_in` call under `generated_entry`. Allocation
calls move from 874 to 873 and peak heap from 118.30 to 114.52 MB (-3.20%).
Temporary allocations move from 164 to 165. The separate 4.20 MiB Deflate
growth bucket remains and is explicitly not claimed as removable.

Instrumented peak RSS falls from 124.09 to 122.64 MB. Uninstrumented GNU Time
ABBA maximum RSS is 116,492/116,236 KiB before and 116,244/116,372 KiB after;
maximum-to-maximum moves -0.10%, treated as flat.

compressible samples per leg:
