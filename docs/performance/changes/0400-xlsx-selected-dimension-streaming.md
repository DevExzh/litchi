# Change 0400: dimension-bearing XLSX selected-cell streaming and numeric scratch

Date: 2026-09-04

Status: accepted scoped production optimization. The retained latency claim is
limited to the fixed filesystem XLSX selected-cell operation and protocol
below. The candidate is a cumulative change: the measured result is not
attributed to either production mechanism in isolation.

`performance_claim: scoped`

`claim_authorized: true`

## Production change and boundary

Production commit `e97d8920f` reuses a private, bounded `String` scratch
buffer for numeric and untyped cell values encountered by the selected-query
scanner, including selected and unselected cells. The scanner takes and clears
the buffer at cell start, recycles it after semantic construction and selection
have completed, and discards capacity above the cell-character limit. Selected
lexical values, including numeric spellings and `-0.000`, are unchanged.

Production commit `f159c0aed` additionally keeps selected queries on the
streaming path when a worksheet has valid SpreadsheetML `dimension` metadata.
The `dimension@ref` is validated and discarded; it does not bound or otherwise
alter selected results. Transitional and strict names, supported prefixed
forms, and MCE-ignorable metadata retain the intended semantics. Missing,
malformed, duplicate, late, or unsupported dimension metadata preserves the
eager-parser error or conservative fallback behavior, including rejection of
unknown attributes and nested non-whitespace content. The public API and
selected-cell output are unchanged.

The comparison is therefore control revision
`2e47ccebf449ef88943c0abcecd32bd9141eb520` versus final candidate revision
`f159c0aed603672aacee8e5923586ce4aa8753f7`. The candidate includes both
`e97d8920f` and `f159c0aed`; no scratch-only causality claim is made.

## Timing protocol and accepted normal result

Each sample used a fresh child process and retained both the selected worksheet
handle and selected-cell view through the snapshots. Public
`litchi::Workbook::open(path)` and the mixed-case selector/address strings were
prepared before the allocation region and `Instant` start. The timer includes
only case-insensitive worksheet selection followed by the exact `M29` cell
read. Measurements were warm-only normal, non-allocator release binaries,
with CPU affinity 2, one worker, 20 warmups, 500 retained samples per leg,
and strict `A1(control) / B1(candidate) / B2(candidate) / A2(control)` order.
Rust/Cargo/Rustdoc 1.98.1 was pinned explicitly because the repository's 1.95
installation does not include Cargo.

The percentages below are exact candidate reductions from the raw samples;
positive means the candidate is faster. Every listed statistic passed the
paired direction and stability gates.

## Correctness and reproducibility

Focused tests cover valid scalar, range, empty, explicit-close, absolute,
strict-prefixed, and MCE-ignorable dimensions; missing, invalid, inverted,
out-of-grid, duplicate, late, unknown-attribute, nested-content, and combined
metadata refusal/fallback cases; source/eager parity; and preservation of
unmaterialized selected source cells until a later materializing query. Long-
to-short scratch reuse, signed zero, exponent spelling, selected and
unselected cells, scalar transitions, entity/CDATA, oversize capacity, and
malformed-tail paths are covered as well.

The full `litchi-xlsx --lib` suite passed **918/918**; focused selected and
source/fallback checks passed **9/9** and **3/3** (the complete `0400` filter
passed **12/12**), the unified-facade checks passed **4/4**, and the
`cargo check -p litchi-xlsx` gate passed under Rust 1.98.1. Exact changed-file
rustfmt and the `git diff --check` gate passed. The warning-denied all-target
Clippy command remains
non-clean: the pinned control reports 26 diagnostics and the candidate reports
28, including two new test-only `result_large_err` diagnostics in the 0400 test
helpers. This does not affect the production-library or full-test results.

The [0400 evidence bundle](../results/change-0400/) retains the raw normal and
allocator reports, corpus identity, binary provenance, metric summaries, and
adjudication. Initial zero-effect screens from before dimension support and
contaminated or otherwise invalid captures were rejected and are excluded
from the bundle.

## Authorized claim and exclusions

`performance_claim: scoped`; `claim_authorized: true`. The authorized claim is
only the normal, non-allocator release-binary latency of case-insensitive
selection of `bEnCh01` followed by the exact stored-number read at `M29`, over
the fixed medium filesystem XLSX corpus and the stated CPU-2, one-worker,
fresh-child, warm-only ABBA protocol. It is a cumulative result for the
dimension-bearing streaming and reusable numeric-scratch implementation; no
scratch-only attribution follows.

No claim follows for allocator-enabled elapsed time; allocator peak/RSS,
total-memory, or system-memory behavior; physical I/O or locality; cold or
cold-verified behavior; throughput; workbook-open time outside the timer;
other cells, selectors, corpora, XLSX workloads, facades, formats, or broad
generalization.
