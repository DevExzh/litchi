# Change 0228: DOCX-family layout noise floor calibration (methodology)

Date: 2026-08-20

## Purpose

Not a code change — a measurement-methodology calibration, the DOCX-family
analog of 0223/0226 (themselves continuing 0205 ODS / 0213 ODP / 0218 ODT).
The OOXML family had NO calibrated floors: 0205/0213/0218/0223/0226 all
calibrated ODF crates, and 0187 (the last DOCX-affecting measurement
change) predates the guardrail/floor convention entirely. The pivot to
DOCX work means the first DOCX optimization (an 0209/0224-style fused
open parse or an 0217/0225-style text-path change in litchi-docx) would
hit the pre-floor rule on its very first adverse-both guardrail reading —
the exact situation that forced the reactive 0223 and 0226 calibrations.
0228 calibrates the likely guardrail phases BEFORE that change, so its
verdict is not blocked.

## Phase set

Byte-identical-guardrail-shaped phases for a litchi-docx-confined first
change — the open/lifecycle phases a text-path change never executes, and
that still carry the most layout-sensitive open code for an open-path
change (0223 precedent: file phases calibrated ahead of semantic ones):

| Selector | Role |
|---|---|
| `docx_file_source_open` | guardrail (open path, source-backed) |
| `docx_file_eager_open` | guardrail (open path, eager; the most layout-sensitive open shape per 0223's ODT finding) |
| `docx_file_source_open_full_text_lifecycle` | guardrail (open + full-text lifecycle) |
| `docx_file_eager_open_full_text_lifecycle` | guardrail (eager variant) |
| `xlsx_file_open` | cross-guardrail (OOXML substrate sibling; the 0187 selector) |
| `pptx_file_source_open` | cross-guardrail (OOXML substrate sibling) |

Cross-guardrail rationale: ODT changes (0224/0225/0227) carried exactly
two cross-family guardrails on the shared ODF substrate. For a
litchi-docx change the shared substrate is litchi-opc +
litchi-ooxml-common and the sibling owners are litchi-xlsx and
litchi-pptx. Out of scope: `docx_semantic_*` phases (executed phases of
the likely first change, not guardrails — same scoping as 0226). If a
first change needs semantic-phase floors, that is a follow-up
calibration.

## Probes

All probes build from the identical banked tree (control SHA-256
`1d503363657f7badb0d2c321ca208dbe9e9cbf22765745fc8ccd1a1a7ab1e1cd`, the
banked post-0227 binary; source revision 3ab0bbd1b); each probe module
was removed after its build and the tree verified to rebuild bit-exact
to the control SHA. Probe functions are `#[inline(never)]` parser-shaped
byte-dispatch bodies generated by the 0223 generator
(`/tmp/perf-0192/0223/gen_padding.py`, seeds 2281-2283) with
per-function distinct constants (defeating identical-code folding),
retained via a `#[used]` function-pointer table; nothing calls them, so
no probe instruction executes on any measured path.

| Probe | Placement | Added text bytes | SHA-256 |
|---|---|---:|---|
| a | `litchi-docx` (11 fns) | +3,872 | `5a08ca1283dc29304849c1cdd7f5958b3c187d651346af3b9bcabc3a84a851a4` |
| b | `litchi-docx` (17 fns) | +5,984 | `4a0afd55b75f2c8c286cfe41973c2ce430185509fc6534455c4f3a190ae8459e` |
| c | `litchi-docx` (22 fns) | +7,824 | `f191a309af97cd1fad059ef82015afab762f574ce7edf8d52a02d28d46c8b2ca` |

The generator only adds code, so the probes bracket a first change's
perturbation in magnitude, not sign (0226 wording); the expected
first-change shifts are the 0224 class (fused open parse: +848 B .text)
through the 0225 class (text-path memo: −6,064 B), both inside the
ladder's magnitude range.

Protocol: identical to the change-measurement protocol — frozen
binaries, fresh CPU-2-pinned processes, order A1 control / B1 probe /
B2 probe / A2 control, 30 warmups, 500 samples per leg, drift ceilings
5%/5%/10%/15% (p50/mean/p95/p99, absolute value); 72 legs total
(6 phases × 3 probes × 4 legs). Because control and probe are
semantically identical, EVERY paired-direction reading is pure layout
noise; the observed magnitudes calibrate the floor.

## Drift-rejected pairs and the rerun protocol

Five phase/probe pairs failed same-implementation drift on their control
legs (A1→A2). Per protocol each got ONE permitted rerun (legs r1..r4,
drift r1 vs r4). **All five failed drift again** and are EXCLUDED from
floor consolidation: their summaries are recorded with decision
`drift-rejected-excluded` (built from the original a1/b1/b2/a2 legs as
evidence; the rerun legs are audit-only and fold into nothing), their
statistics stay uncalibrated ("-"), and the pre-floor rule applies.

| Pair | Original rejection | Rerun failure (r1 vs r4) | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pptx_file_source_open` a | warm p95 +65.4%, p99 +277.9%; cold-requested p95 +89.6%, p99 +709.9% | cold-requested p50 +570.4%, mean +653.0%, p95 +1686.8% | cold-requested page-cache bimodality |
| `docx_file_source_open_full_text_lifecycle` b | cold-requested p99 +23.4% | cold-requested p50 +75.1%, mean +27.8% | same bimodality |
| `docx_file_source_open` b | negative drift (A1 noise-inflated): warm p99 −43.4%; cold-requested p50 −7.1%, mean −16.4%, p95 −22.6%, p99 −55.8% | cold-requested p50 +91.9%, mean +19.9%, p99 +27.0% | same bimodality |
| `docx_file_source_open_full_text_lifecycle` c | cold-requested p99 +57.0% | cold-requested p50 −77.7%, mean −81.4%, p95 −92.0%, p99 −95.0% (opposite pole: r1 was the inflated leg) | same bimodality |
| `xlsx_file_open` c | p50 +5.7%, mean +5.1% (marginal) | p50 +11.9%, mean +10.1%, p95 +10.4% | ordinary machine noise on a single-row phase, persistent across both attempts |

**Page-cache bimodality finding.** Four of the five rejections share one
signature: the cold-requested row is BIMODAL on this machine. The
cache-eviction request sometimes genuinely misses and sometimes does
not; when it misses, the whole distribution shifts 2-7x (pptx probe-a
control legs: a1/a2/r1 cold p50 ≈ 6.1-6.4 ms vs r4 41.0 ms). Warm rows
were reproducible across all four control legs of every rejected pair
(pptx warm p50 2.89-3.01 ms), but exclusion is pair-level per protocol.
Implication: **cold-requested rows are currently uncalibratable at the
5/5/10/15% drift ceilings on this machine** for source-open shapes.
Calibrating them needs either a controlled page-cache protocol or wider
cold ceilings; deferred until a change's verdict actually requires it.

`docx_file_eager_open` a/b/c,
`docx_file_source_open_full_text_lifecycle` a,
`docx_file_eager_open_full_text_lifecycle` a/b/c,
`xlsx_file_open` a/b, `pptx_file_source_open` b/c.

## Floor rule (verbatim from 0223)

For each statistic, collect every paired reading where BOTH directions
are adverse (reduction < 0 in both a1→b1 and a2→b2). Probe readings and
historical adverse-both readings on byte-identical phases fold into the
same maximum (the 0205/0213/0218 method). The floor is the worst
observed adverse magnitude — max over readings of max(|d1|, |d2|) — with
NO additional margin, displayed at one decimal. Statistics with no
adverse-both evidence stay uncalibrated and the pre-floor rule still
applies to them.

There is no historical fold-in yet: the first DOCX change's
byte-identical-phase adverse-both readings fold into these tables when
it runs.

+3.9 KB to +7.8 KB; substantially larger deltas, other crates, or
protocol changes require recalibration.

## Consequences

The first DOCX optimization change now runs with calibrated guardrails:
any adverse-both reading within these floors on a byte-identical phase
is a layout reading under rule 1, and statistics still marked "-" fall
under the pre-floor rule (a residual adverse-both reading on an
uncalibrated statistic triggers a targeted follow-up calibration, the
0225→0226 pattern). The 0229 text-path change (`extract_word_text`
binding-tracker port) is the first consumer: its byte-identical
guardrails are exactly the four docx phases above, with the two OOXML
cross-guardrails on the shared substrate.

Raw artifacts: `docs/performance/results/{slug}-0228{a,b,c}-*` for the
six slugs (per-leg JSON compressed, summaries, manifests). Rerun legs
(`-r{1..4}`) are audit-only and remain outside the repository.
