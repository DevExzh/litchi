## 0638 — the ordinary documented save is publication, not serialization; and the facade's legacy routes finally have selectors

Evidence gap 5 of change [0587](0587-remaining-opportunity-survey.md) had two halves — *"no harness selector opens `.doc` or `.ppt` through the facade"* and *"missing: … the ordinary OOXML save"* — and change [0630](0630-queue-refresh-after-the-first-wave.md) confirmed both were still open after the first wave. Thirty opt-in selectors close them, none in `Case::DEFAULT`: six over `litchi::Document::open` / `litchi::Presentation::open` on a caller-named `--ole2-file` fixture, and twenty-four over the documented `Package::save` / `Workbook::save` route in **four separately reported phases** — lifecycle, semantic edit, atomic save-to-path (change [0497](changes/0497-docx-atomic-publication.md)'s interval: sibling creation, write, permission preservation, `sync_all`, `rename`, parent-directory sync) and documented sequential serialization into a bounded counting sink — over three existing harness corpora and change [0593](0593-opc-publication-pristine-members.md)'s own three real fixtures. **The headline is the phase decomposition.** On six corpora the atomic publication costs **7.7× to 61.7×** the identical serialization: 5.16 ms against 0.32 (DOCX generated), 5.53 against 0.09 (DOCX real), 11.58 against 1.50 (XLSX generated), 7.65 against 0.30 (XLSX real), 5.25 against 0.09 (PPTX generated), 6.89 against 0.27 (PPTX real). A documented save is **publication-bound**, and a compression or layout change on this route is worth at most 0.2–8.7% of a lifecycle. Change 0593 saw this once (*"inside floor; route is fsync-bound"*) and could not act on it; it is now six corpora on registered selectors. **Second finding: one shape-text edit on a real 108 KB deck costs 133.61 ms** — 95.7% of the PPTX real-file lifecycle, **490×** that deck's complete serialization, and **68.7×** the same edit on the 61-member generated corpus — with a 3.02% A/A spread and no explanation; `opened_presentation_transaction()` captures the whole opened presentation and nothing has ever profiled that capture. **Third: the byte split prices 0593's pristine-member preservation in bytes for the first time** — a one-element edit regenerates **0.45%** of the XLSX real file's 632,699 payload bytes, **1.9%** of the PPTX real file's and 13.1% of the generated DOCX's, while ZIP framing alone is 19,658 B (3.0%) of the 131-member XLSX output. **Fourth, on the facade:** a DOC open costs 15.09 µs at 9,728 B and 178.63 µs at 335,360 B while a PPT open is flat (25.55 → 25.95 µs for 3.2× the bytes); a *refused* DOC open still costs 14.37 µs, a complete open; and on the 335 KB fixture `paragraph_text` of a 55-character paragraph costs **1.96× `text()` of all 9,017 characters** (538.31 against 275.32 µs; 3.7× on the marginal query after a shared 178.63 µs open), which is worth an instruction profile. **An admission fact worth a queue row:** `litchi_docx::Package::document_mut()` refuses change 0593's own `alt-chunk-header.docx` with *"invalid DOCX XML: syntax error: tag not closed"* although `Package::open` admits it and `Package::save` republishes it — one fixture, stable over 600 retained samples, uncharacterized, and a `crates/litchi-docx` question rather than a harness one. A/A floor over three full repeats: **p50 2.09%** across 39 rows, with 8 rows above 5% reported by name — five of them carry the atomic publication on a device shared with seven agents, so **every atomic median is an order of magnitude, not a number**; the *ratios* are robust. `performance_claim: none`; no file under `crates/` changed; registry 471 → 501 names, `Case::DEFAULT` stays 41, checked default catalog SHA-256 unmoved. OLE2/OOXML remain active; ODF stays deferred until completion and iWork is excluded. [Record and limitations](0638-facade-and-ordinary-save-selectors.md); [retained evidence](results/change-0638/README.md).

## 0638 — the entry points `docs/GOAL.md`'s definition of done is about had never been measured by a selector

Record: [0638](0638-facade-and-ordinary-save-selectors.md).

`docs/GOAL.md`'s definition of done asks for reproducible baselines (clause a), work proportional to accessed content (c) and the avoidance of parsing or recompressing unrelated content (d) — and until this record **none of those clauses had ever been evaluated on the entry points a caller actually reaches for**: `litchi::Document::open`, `litchi::Presentation::open`, `Package::save` and `Workbook::save`. Change 0587 recorded the gap; changes 0593, 0607 and 0609 each had to build a throwaway probe outside the workspace to say anything about these routes, and 0609's 57-fixture DOC census has been unreproducible from the repository ever since. Clause (a) is now satisfied for all four: thirty registered selectors, checked in, with a frozen oracle per facade scenario and a frozen publication digest per save corpus. Clause (d) gets its first byte-denominated answer on the documented save — a one-element edit regenerates 0.45% to 13.1% of the payload and leaves the rest byte-identical — but the answer arrives with the finding that it barely matters, because the publication costs 7.7× to 61.7× the serialization it would shrink. Clause (c) gets a counter-example on the facade: on a 335 KB `.doc`, reading *one* 55-character paragraph costs 1.96× reading all 9,017 characters, which is work **inversely** proportional to accessed content. Three tiers, stated as the audit requires: the request-free counts — output bytes, payload provenance, member counts, framing, edit outcome and publication digest — are **measured** and identical across all 5,850 retained samples of all three repeats; the wall-clock phase medians are **measured** but five of thirty-nine rows sit on a shared device with an A/A floor up to 60.36%, so their magnitudes are reported and their precision is not claimed; and the cause of the 133.61 ms PPTX opened-transaction edit is **unknown** and deliberately left so, because no instruction profile of `opened_presentation_transaction()` exists. Two audit-relevant facts the record produces without proposing anything. First, the documented DOCX editor declines a real fixture its own reader admits and its own writer republishes, which is the first evidence that Path A's *editor* — not its reader and not its writer — is the narrow gate, and which change 0593's Limitations section predicted in words (*"no example opens a real `.docx`, edits through the model and saves"*). Second, the facade's PPT open is flat in file size while its DOC open is not, a shape difference no in-memory selector had made visible. What this batch does not discharge: gap 5 also names OLE2 length-changing saves, file-backed cold part reads, PPTX capture/commit/apply timers and eager DOCX phases, and those stay open; there is no cold-cache, physical-device or `fsync`-attribution result, no allocation or instruction count, one edit shape per format, and five real fixtures. `performance_claim: none`; no file under `crates/` changed. OLE2/OOXML remain the active priority; ODF stays deferred; iWork is excluded.

## 0638 — two new observation axes, one new bounded input, and no boundary moved

Record: [0638](0638-facade-and-ordinary-save-selectors.md).

No production code changed, so no ADR boundary moved and no accepted hash changed. The two new modules reach the library exclusively through documented public entry points — `litchi::Document::open`/`text`/`paragraph_text`, `litchi::Presentation::open`/`text`/`slide`, `litchi_docx::Package::open`/`document_mut`/`save`/`to_stream`, `litchi_xlsx::Workbook::open`/`edit`/`save`/`write_to`, `litchi_pptx::Package::open`/`opened_presentation_transaction`/`apply_opened_presentation_commit`/`save`/`to_bytes` — and no typed error, limit, refusal point, validation order, output byte or public API is reachable from them. **ADR 0005's mandatory-validation clause is observed, not altered**: every validation those entry points perform — format detection and read limits on both facade opens, OPC validation on `Package::open`, `Workbook::from_package`'s complete workbook/relationship validation, `opened_presentation`'s topology capture, and the destination symlink refusal and permission preservation inside `litchi_opc::atomic::replace_with` — lies inside the measured region of the phase that invokes it, and the two typed refusals the baseline met are reported verbatim and measured rather than dropped, which is the treatment change 0627 gave the XLS full-text and PPT one-shape refusals. **ADR 0006's determinism clause is asserted rather than assumed**: changes 0625 and 0631 made the OLE2 writer's storage order and three OOXML relationship-order verdict sites functions of the document, and this record proves the consequence on the route a caller uses — six corpora each prove, before any sample runs, that two fresh open/edit/save cycles publish the same digest and that saving one edited owner twice publishes the same digest, and every one of 5,850 retained samples then reproduced its corpus's digest and its frozen edit outcome. **ADR 0003's bounded-resource rule is satisfied additively**: `--ooxml-file` introduces a **new** 32 MiB ceiling checked from `fs::metadata` before the module's single read of a caller-named path, matching `--real-file`'s and `--ole2-file`'s; the counting sink is bounded and refuses to grow past its budget; and the private save workspace is created under the caller's `--filesystem-root` and removed on drop, so the destination device is the caller's choice rather than an ambient default. `--ole2-file` gains a `WordDocument` arm and a shared `classify_inputs`, which **widens** what the flag accepts and changes no existing selector's behaviour, corpus identity or error text. No new `unsafe`, no `#[global_allocator]`, no hidden global Rayon pool, no `std::env` and no `Command` in either new module — the one place `std::env` is reached is `filesystem.rs`, which already owned it, through a `scratch_root(root, label)` factored out of the existing `filesystem_root`; five source-policy tests in `tools/test_perf_baseline_source_policy.py` assert all of that, following the three change 0627 added. The default matrix is frozen: all thirty variants are absent from `Case::DEFAULT`, a fifth policy test asserts it by name, and `test_corpus_manifest_v2` and `test_crud_coverage_index` both pass, which is the mechanical statement that `catalog_sha256` and `content_set_sha256` did not move. Five generator identities — `litchi-doc-facade-real-file-v1`, `litchi-ppt-facade-real-file-v1`, `litchi-docx-real-file-v1`, `litchi-xlsx-real-file-save-v1`, `litchi-pptx-real-file-v1` — are documented in `docs/performance/CORPUS_MANIFEST_V2.md` and deliberately **not** added to the family map, for the reason changes 0601 and 0627 gave: a caller-named file's licensing and provenance cannot be asserted by a static map. The harness's `litchi` dependency gains its `doc` and `ppt` features; both leaf crates were already direct dependencies, so the lock gains two dependency lines and no package. One compliance-relevant observation the record makes without acting on it: `litchi_docx::Package::document_mut()` refuses a real fixture that `Package::open` admits and `Package::save` republishes, which is a `crates/litchi-docx` question and is reported, not fixed. `performance_claim: none`. OLE2/OOXML remain active; ODF stays deferred until completion and iWork is excluded.
