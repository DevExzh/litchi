## Disposition

The accepted proof path is conservative by inspection. The shared `NsReader` and raw parser remain semantic authorities; the strict validator excludes dependency-bearing worksheet elements; formulas in the source disable the proof; and the proof checks source row/cell order, parser-resolved inferred addresses, cardinality, tag decoding, dimensions, and source bounds before publication. I found no accepted-path residual that bypasses a full-scanner guard, and no source/Store pairing defect in the current production ownership path.

Admission remains held for the following concrete reasons:

1. The allocation concern from the moving-source review is **reclassified after comparing the frozen draft with the baseline**. `tag`, `cell_tag`, `CellSlot` primary-box creation, and `into_boxed_slice` are already used by the complete scanner (`scan.rs` captures them for every cell and dimension), while the compact route calls the same helpers only for a changed cell and, when needed, the dimension ([sheet_data.rs](candidate-sources/crates/litchi-xlsx/src/raw/worksheet/edit/codec/snapshot/write/sheet_data.rs#L285), [sheet_data.rs](candidate-sources/crates/litchi-xlsx/src/raw/worksheet/edit/codec/snapshot/write/sheet_data.rs#L447)). The source byte cap remains 8 MiB and the event cap remains 131,072. `MAX_PROOF_BYTES` is a logical retained-proof-cache cap, not the complete writer/output budget. I found no newly unbounded allocation or weakened input bound here: the new tag materialization is one source-size-bounded temporary per changed cell, and the action-map clone is bounded by the public 256-action limit. It does add transient duplicate attribute decoding for changed cell tags (the proof decodes all tags, then the writer decodes changed tags again), so a worst-case large-cell/tag allocation and timing measurement is still required, but this inherited materialization is not by itself an admission blocker.

2. The proof metadata accounting is improved in the frozen draft: `FIXED_METADATA_BYTES` is charged up front, row/cell buffers use checked geometric capacity growth, and a populated scratch-drop test exists ([source_proof.rs](candidate-sources/crates/litchi-xlsx/src/cell_values/source_proof.rs#L100), [source_proof.rs](candidate-sources/crates/litchi-xlsx/src/cell_values/source_proof.rs#L198), [source_proof.rs](candidate-sources/crates/litchi-xlsx/src/cell_values/source_proof.rs#L830), [source_proof.rs](candidate-sources/crates/litchi-xlsx/src/cell_values/source_proof.rs#L1132)). Attribute normalization in `decode_tag`/`attribute_value`, validator name/text copies, and the compact writer's temporary tags are outside that logical cache charge, as they are outside the complete writer's retained proof representation as well. The cap boundary should be stated and the transient duplicate decode measured; it is not evidence of a newly weakened source/resource bound.

3. `compact_plan_eligible` rejects removals and shared-formula payloads, but otherwise admits every `Action::Update`, including `SharedString` and future payload variants ([package.rs](candidate-sources/crates/litchi-xlsx/src/raw/worksheet/edit/package.rs#L245)). The current public source-backed route constructs only `Set`, `ClearIfPresent`, `Remove`, and shared-formula actions; the latter two unsupported cases are rejected before the compact writer, so this is not an observed public-path failure. Keep the compact entry point private and enforce/document its exact action contract, with a direct regression for any intentionally supported action, before treating the proof as a stable internal boundary.

4. The private proof tests now compare compact and complete bytes, omission spans, and reduced readback for representative prefixed, inferred, empty, typed, CDATA, dimension, formula-refusal, source-mismatch, Store-mismatch, malformed-attribute, and cap cases ([source_proof.rs](candidate-sources/crates/litchi-xlsx/src/cell_values/source_proof.rs#L878)). The public guards exercise semantic ZIP behavior and historical errors, but they do not provide a public exact-byte oracle for accepted compact rewrites. The campaign is still missing an accepted `SetFormula` on a source with no formula, public Clear/style and multi/discontiguous existing-cell combinations, normalization-heavy tags, large-tag/cell timing and allocation evidence, and aggregate cap/cancellation behavior. Cloned snapshots, inverse/re-edit, empty-sheet insertion, and Store/source mismatch have guards, but the frozen draft still needs the targeted build and differential campaign from 0551 before admission.

## Checks covered by the current snapshot

- **Full-scanner residuals and latent errors.** The strict validator limits the proof grammar to the worksheet envelope, views, defaults, columns, dimensions, rows, cells, and scalar payload elements. MCE, `x14ac`, `dyDescent`, merge/protection/validation, and other dependency-bearing forms are excluded or cause authoritative fallback. The ordinary parser is finalized before proof publication, and completed parser errors retain the existing source parse path. All Start/Empty attributes are UTF-8 checked and XML 1.0 decoded/normalized, including namespace and otherwise unused attributes; the public malformed `spans`/namespace cases preserve the complete error. Empty `<cols>` refuses the proof and preserves the complete scanner error; empty `<sheetData/>` is retained exactly and cannot enter the compact mutation plan.

- **Strict and inferred address association.** The handoff runs after parser transitions. Row and cell addresses therefore come from parser-resolved state, including inferred `r` values, rather than a second decoder. The proof requires strictly increasing source rows and columns; `finish` compares compact slot count with the parser Store; the plan walks every slot against the same Store entry order; and the new `entries_ptr` plus `entries_len` check rejects a foreign Store slice ([model.rs](candidate-sources/crates/litchi-xlsx/src/raw/worksheet/edit/codec/snapshot/model.rs#L209), [source_proof.rs](candidate-sources/crates/litchi-xlsx/src/cell_values/source_proof.rs#L768)). `compact_layout_offsets_valid` also checks source, row, cell, sheetData, and dimension span ordering before an offset is used ([package.rs](candidate-sources/crates/litchi-xlsx/src/raw/worksheet/edit/package.rs#L196)).

- **Changed tags and provenance.** The changed-cell reader enables end-name checks, lazily materializes only the changed cell tag, preserves the original prefix and attribute order, and removes/replaces only the address/type/style attributes and primary payload that the ordinary writer would change. Unchanged spans are copied from the immutable source. Dimension expansion unions all retained Store entries and action-created addresses, matching the complete writer's relevant bounds. Exact private differentials cover several tag forms; unusual attribute and payload forms still need the broader oracle listed above.

- **Source ownership and invalidation.** The proof is published from the parser-owned `Store` and retained beside the immutable `SourcePayload` in `Snapshot`; production commit borrows the proof, source bytes, and `Snapshot` Store entries together. `SourcePayload` and the `Arc<Store>` keep both allocations alive, the pointer/length checks run before offsets, and every worksheet payload replacement clears `compact_proof` ([snapshot.rs](candidate-sources/crates/litchi-xlsx/src/cell_values/snapshot.rs#L447), [snapshot.rs](candidate-sources/crates/litchi-xlsx/src/cell_values/snapshot.rs#L700), [snapshot.rs](candidate-sources/crates/litchi-xlsx/src/cell_values/snapshot.rs#L1046)). The source lineage/version checks and patch conflict path remain separate authority checks. I do not require an additional owning wrapper for the current private production path; retain the clone, source-mismatch, Store-mismatch, and re-edit regressions.

- **Refusal and resource behavior.** Proof refusal disables and drops its scratch state while the validator/parser can finish or the caller can run the established complete path. The candidate now uses fixed metadata charging and checked geometric row/cell growth rather than the earlier per-eight-cell reserve pattern. Output and omission buffers use fallible reserve calls. The writer-side tag allocations are inherited from the complete scanner's tag model and remain outside the logical proof-cache cap; their transient duplicate decode and peak timing still need runtime evidence. No runtime peak or allocation-failure evidence is available here.

## Required evidence before the status can change

The candidate needs a stable post-compile source hash, a targeted build, exact compact-versus-complete output/error differentials for accepted and refused forms, and the representative OLE2/OOXML performance and retained-memory evidence required by the 0551 checklist. Until those artifacts exist and the action/coverage gaps are closed, this review must not be recorded as a pass.
