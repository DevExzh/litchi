# Track C survey: CFB FAT geometry and BIFF globals read schedules

All read counts below are a **model of stream-level (logical) reads** derived from
each file's CFB and BIFF geometry, mirroring `parse_globals` in
`crates/litchi-xls/src/workbook/source.rs` (today: one 4-byte header read per globals
record including EOF, abort at FILEPASS before its payload, then one bulk range read of
`[0, globals_end)`) and the single-pass schedule of change 0565: an exact prologue of the
first 4 records, each costing one header read and one payload read, then fills of 512 bytes
doubling to 65536, every fill clamped by the stream length, by `max_global_bytes`, and by the
smallest BoundSheet8 `lbPlyPos` seen so far. The `phys` columns count the physically contiguous
spans those logical reads would split into under `SharedOleFile`'s run grouping. **Nothing
here is a measured syscall count.** Generated by `survey.py`; per-file records are in
`survey.json`.

Scope: 104 files under `test-data/ole/`; 98 opened as CFB; 6 skipped (listed at the end).
Appendix: 72 `*.xls` fixtures elsewhere under `test-data/` (POI, LibreOffice, interop), surveyed the same way.
