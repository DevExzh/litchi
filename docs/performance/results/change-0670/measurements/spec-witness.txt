Local primary schema witness

The local ECMA-376 Strict schema archive is
`3rdparty/specs/ECMA-376/ECMA-376-1_5th_edition_december_2016.zip`.

After extracting its strict schema bundle:

  wml.xsd:3233-3238 defines CT_Body as EG_BlockLevelElts* followed by an
  optional, single sectPr. A body-final sectPr is therefore schema-valid only
  as the final body child; a sectPr followed by another body child remains
  outside that content model.

  shared-commonSimpleTypes.xsd:57-59 defines ST_TwipsMeasure as the union of
  ST_UnsignedDecimalNumber and ST_PositiveUniversalMeasure.

  shared-commonSimpleTypes.xsd:95-103 defines ST_UniversalMeasure and its
  positive restriction with the lexical suffixes mm, cm, in, pt, pc, and pi.

The reader and writer section tests use 612pt/792pt page dimensions and pt
margins, columns, and grid pitch. The original section XML remains byte-exact
on the reader source-preserving round trip.
