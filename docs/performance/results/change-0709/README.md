# 0709 DOCX ordinary-save baseline packet

This packet records a current-source descriptive baseline for the documented
ordinary DOCX open, semantic edit, and save route at revision
`9b6a9bbfc7bcc2fbf103419eb6968e1025c529c7`. It contains no production
candidate and carries `performance_claim: none`. The only source change is the
testable real-file corpus metadata correction in
`tools/perf-baseline/src/ordinary_save.rs`; the DOCX production crates are
unchanged. iWork is excluded.

The matrix contains one deterministic generated medium corpus (200
paragraphs), the admitted checked-in `NumberedList.docx`, and the refused
checked-in `alt-chunk-header.docx`. The real files are bound by the fixture
paths, sizes, and hashes in [`plan.json`](plan.json) and
[`fixtures.json`](fixtures.json). The first file carries self-reported Office
metadata, but this packet makes no native Office producer or compatibility
claim.

## Capture contract

The native lane has three repeats, 100 measured samples, and 10 warmups. The
allocator lane has two repeats, three samples, and no warmup. Children are
pinned to CPU 12. Repeat two reverses both corpus and phase order. Every `(corpus, phase,
repeat, lane)` is a separate child, and every child records source custody,
binary identity, fixture identity, output identity, edit outcome, and semantic
publication evidence.

The four phases are independent intervals: `lifecycle` (open, edit, and save),
`edit` (`Owner::edit`: `document_mut` admission and marker append),
`atomic_publish` (documented save-to-path, including sync and rename), and
`counting_publish` (documented sequential serialization to a bounded counting
sink). Phase distributions are descriptive and are not additive. The atomic
phase includes filesystem sync costs; it is not a physical cold-cache or
hardware-device claim. Allocator elapsed values are retained as instrumentation
evidence and are not latency-comparable to native timing.

## Correction custody

The preflight build discovered that real-file manifest fields used compressed
ZIP member bytes and hash where decoded main-part identity was required. The
corrected harness reads the decoded main member through bounded archive limits
and computes the aggregate uncompressed member total with checked arithmetic.
The correction and its focused tests are recorded in
[`metadata-correction.json`](metadata-correction.json),
[`harness-metadata.patch`](harness-metadata.patch), and
[`harness-change.json`](harness-change.json). Preflight builds and one-sample
admission witnesses are retained under
[`metadata-preflight`](metadata-preflight) and are explicitly excluded from
formal measurement. Formal binaries are rebuilt after the correction.

The independent semantic oracle checks the admitted fixture through both
public publication doors (`to_stream` and `save`): prior paragraphs remain an
exact prefix and the fixed marker occurs exactly once as the final paragraph.
Those scoped admitted-fixture semantic checks pass. They do not certify
whole-package relationship or unknown-markup preservation; the admitted
rewrite also changes decoded `word/_rels/document.xml.rels`. The strict oracle remains failed
for the refused fixture because both publication doors rewrite decoded
`docProps/custom.xml` even though no custom-property edit was requested.
Source investigation attributes this to DOCX `write_plain` unconditionally
calling
[`custom_props.write_for`](../../../../crates/litchi-docx/src/package/codec.rs:1194)
while the custom-properties state is clean; the canonical encoder changes the
fixture's BOM and `op:` prefix form. The
strict failure is retained under `oracle/first-semantic` and is not weakened
into a correctness pass. The decoded custom-properties member changes from
632 to 602 bytes, and it is not equal after ASCII-whitespace removal. Both
no-edit controls produce the same archive SHA-256 as their refused-edit
counterparts for both `to_stream` and `save`, while the main document member
and semantic text remain intact. The v2 report and retained artifacts are in
`oracle/report.json` and `oracle/artifacts/`. Compressed-member comparisons
are diagnostics about payload identity only; they do not prove a production
copy-through path.

## Result status

The authoritative result is [`analysis.json`](analysis.json), generated by
[`analyze.py`](analyze.py) after all 36 native and 24 allocator child receipts
(60 total) pass custody, vector, semantic parity, and deterministic-output
checks. This analysis passes those checks and recomputes the formal statistics.
Native repeat review retains 18 over-5-percent spread flags. Allocator repeat
review retains 24 over-5-percent flags on instrumented elapsed p50/mean/p95/p99
statistics; allocation counts and operation-relative live/peak metrics are
reported separately. Absolute process gauges and phase peaks are not used for
cross-phase or cross-process conclusions, and phase values are never summed.
Harness
parity does not establish full workflow preservation, because the strict
oracle currently fails on the clean custom-properties rewrite described above.
The separate profile analysis passes all eight Callgrind profiles. It measures
`Owner::edit` (including `document_mut` admission and marker append) and
`write_plain` for two repeats on the generated and admitted real corpora;
aggregate owner rows are 7,224,625/7,268,758 Ir and 2,685,230/2,684,009 Ir for
edit, and 7,248,464/7,232,750 Ir and 3,047,180/3,046,831 Ir for publication.
Setup and termination parts are excluded, immediate direct children reconcile,
and the nested `document_mut` diagnostic is not added to that partition. The
profile binary remains bound to source revision `9b6a9bbfc7bcc2fbf103419eb6968e1025c529c7`;
the scripts-only checkout transition is recorded in
[`profile-revision-transition.json`](profile-revision-transition.json).
Callgrind Ir is guest-instruction attribution, not native latency, hardware
cycles, allocation counts, RSS, cache counters, or a causal optimization
claim. This packet makes no speedup, RSS, native Office, cold storage,
cross-platform, or producer compatibility claim.
