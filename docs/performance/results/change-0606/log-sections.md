## HOTSPOTS.md

## 0606 — PPT record tree borrowed payloads

Retained. `Record::parse_impl` copied each record's payload into an owned `Vec` and then parsed that record's children from the same bytes, so a container held a private copy of every byte its children also copied: measured across all 30 `.ppt` fixtures the owned payload bytes are 1.00x to 2.35x the `PowerPoint Document` stream, 2.0x or more on 20 of the 26 that parse. `Record::data` becomes a `RecordPayload` that is either an owned `Vec` or a 32-byte span of the `Arc<Vec<u8>>` the `Presentation` already retains, and `parse_document_with_limits` no longer extracts every slide's text at every open. On `45543.ppt` (311,524-byte stream, 286 records): eager open 613 → 304 allocations, 934,751 → 429,870 allocated bytes, 893,053 → 387,876 retained bytes (2.87x → 1.24x the stream), 948.7 → 653.4 `memcpy` calls, 538 → 235 `malloc` calls, 259 → 0 `Record::extract_text` calls, 1,967,863 → 1,496,895 `Ir` (−23.93%); single-shot native 4,399,332 → 3,689,782 instructions and 545 → 419 minor faults. Paired p50: `ppt_semantic_open` −14.92% (floor 1.18%), `ppt_semantic_one_edit_save` −5.33% (floor 1.13%), `ppt_semantic_list_slides` −1.22%, `ppt_semantic_full_text` **+4.71%** (floor 0.32%) because that selector times only the per-slide re-parse, which still runs through the copying `&[u8]` entry point in `SlideFactory`. A 1,000-iteration single-process `open + list slides` loop on the real fixture regresses 105.75% under glibc defaults, entirely from 171,889 versus 768 minor faults: with `MALLOC_MMAP_THRESHOLD_` and `MALLOC_TRIM_THRESHOLD_` pinned at 8 MiB the same loop is −26.07% and the faults disappear, and the single-shot process measurement of the same operation is −14.9% instructions. Next hot loop in this area: `SlideFactory`/`NotesIndex`, which still copy. `performance_claim: none`; no claim is registered. [Change and limitations](0606-ppt-record-tree-borrowed-payloads.md); [retained evidence](results/change-0606/README.md).

## GOAL_AUDIT.md

## 0606 — PPT record tree borrowed payloads

Step 2 of the optimization order (unnecessary copying and allocation) applied to the last unaddressed PPT parser, which change 0301 had explicitly left out of scope. The eager and the source-backed `Presentation` now parse a record tree whose payloads span the `PowerPoint Document` stream the presentation already retains, and the eager per-open full-text extraction that only a unit test consumed is deferred to first use. Retained bytes per byte of stream for an eager open fall from 2.87x to 1.24x on `45543.ppt`, 3.30x to 1.31x on `SampleShow.ppt` and 3.24x to 1.25x on `headers_footers_2007.ppt`; allocations halve. No P1 audit row closes: this is one format's reader, the source-backed PPT path still pays the whole-artifact SHA-256 that 0587 ranked first, `SlideFactory` still copies per slide, and no RSS, cold-cache, physical-device or producer-corpus measurement was taken. Lossless preservation, typed refusals and every record limit are unchanged, and all 30 `.ppt` fixture reader dumps — including the four encrypted refusals — are identical across the two legs. OLE2/OOXML optimization remains active; ODF is deferred until completion and iWork excluded. [Change and limitations](0606-ppt-record-tree-borrowed-payloads.md); [retained evidence](results/change-0606/README.md).

## REPORT.md

## 0606 — PPT record tree borrowed payloads

Retained, `performance_claim: none`. The PPT record parser stops copying every record payload once per nesting level and instead spans the stream the `Presentation` retains; the per-open slide-text extraction becomes lazy. Measured on `45543.ppt`: eager open −23.93% `Ir`, −50.4% allocations, −54.0% allocated bytes, −56.6% retained bytes, −31.1% `memcpy` calls; open plus list slides −21.12% `Ir`; open plus full text −19.17% `Ir`; open, edit and save −12.26% `Ir`. Paired p50 on the registered selectors against an A/A floor of 0.3–1.4%: `ppt_semantic_open` −14.92%, `ppt_semantic_one_edit_save` −5.33%, `ppt_semantic_list_slides` −1.22%, `ppt_semantic_full_text` +4.71%. Two results got worse and are reported, not averaged away: that +4.71%, whose cause is the per-slide re-parse still going through the copying entry point, and a 105.75% regression of a 1,000-iteration single-process `open + list slides` loop on the real fixture, which is a glibc heap-trim artifact of the loop (171,889 versus 768 minor faults; −26.07% and no faults with the glibc thresholds pinned; −14.9% instructions and 128 fewer faults per single-shot process). No speedup, RSS, cold-cache, physical-device or cross-platform claim is registered; the only registered PPT corpus is a 7,680-byte synthetic package, so every real-fixture number comes from a scratch driver retained in the packet. OLE2/OOXML remain active; ODF is deferred until completion and iWork excluded. [Change and limitations](0606-ppt-record-tree-borrowed-payloads.md); [retained evidence](results/change-0606/README.md).

## ADR_COMPLIANCE.md

## 0606 — PPT record tree borrowed payloads

ADR 0003, 0005 and 0006 are all satisfied without a fence change. No byte a reader observes changes: a borrowed payload is the same `data[header_end..record_end]` the old code copied, `PartialEq` and `Debug` compare and render the bytes, and the 30-fixture differential oracle over both readers, the owned text editor's no-op and edited commits and the source-backed editor's reads is identical on both legs once two per-process-randomized `HashMap` `Debug` renderings are normalized (both dumps hash to `9cdddef6e870aa35884738c3f1729c9e12b6ca7bc22b2e551a4dad586c236b7c`). Error identity is preserved exactly, including `max_copied_payload_bytes`: `charge_copy` is still called per record with the same byte count although nothing is copied, so the refusal boundary cannot widen, and a unit test asserts both payload sources accept and refuse at and one byte below the same budget. The strict and lenient container loops, the truncated-container recovery, the byte-at-a-time resynchronization, the depth and record-count ceilings and the four encrypted refusals are unchanged. Deferring the slide-text extraction defers no refusal — `extract_all_text` has no failing branch. Mutation cannot reach the shared stream: `to_mut` follows `Cow::to_mut` and copies a span before returning `&mut`, with a test that edits one record and proves the stream and its siblings unchanged. No new `unsafe` (`#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` holds), no weakened limit or malformed-input defence, no hidden global pool, no ambient I/O, no public leakage of archive types, raw locks or executors. Two `litchi-ppt`-internal public API changes are recorded: `Record::data` is now `RecordPayload`, and `master::model::Element::bytes` loses `const`. Gates: `cargo fmt --all --check` clean, `cargo clippy -p litchi-ppt --all-targets` clean under the deny-level workspace lints, `cargo test -p litchi-ppt` 1,212 passed/0 failed/11 ignored across 32 binaries, `cargo doc -p litchi-ppt --no-deps` clean, `cargo clippy -p litchi -p litchi-pptx --all-targets` clean; the only workspace check failure is four pre-existing `litchi-iwa` chart examples reproduced on the untouched base. `performance_claim: none`. See [Change 0606](0606-ppt-record-tree-borrowed-payloads.md); [retained evidence](results/change-0606/README.md).
