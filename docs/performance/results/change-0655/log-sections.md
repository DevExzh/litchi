## 0655 — the four complete-package hashes of an opened PPTX lifecycle are gone, and the contract they cost has been paid

Record: [0655](0655-pptx-memoized-revision-proof.md).

Change 0590 left "four full-package hashes per lifecycle of the eager selectors" and said removing them "is a durable wire-format change and needs a frozen design record first"; change 0645 froze that design behind nine admission gates; change 0652 decision 4 authorized it in the owner's words — "invalidate the old patches and make the path faster". All nine gates are cleared and recorded. `docs/GOAL.md`'s decision rule is answered on both sides. Worth: **−27.65% of a `pptx_eager_batch_edit_save` lifecycle in instructions**, the fingerprint subtree −74.33%, a memoized fingerprint measured at 0.77% of a cold one. Cost: `LPRM0001` → `LPRM0002` and `LPCP0002` → `LPCP0003`, after which **every patch of either format serialized by an earlier build stops applying**, plus a new public `DurablePatchFormat` enum and a new `Error` variant. GOAL's "never trade a typed refusal for a partial result" shapes the refusal: a superseded magic is refused in `from_bytes`/`from_bytes_with_limits` before any header field is read and with no package in hand, so no caller holds a patch whose public `source_revision() -> [u8; 32]` belongs to a foreign algebra — the alternative, leaving the magic alone, makes an old patch parse and then fail its source check with "the complete package graph differs from the slide-removal patch source", telling the caller the *package* moved when the *proof format* moved. The durable byte limit still runs first, so the malformed-input defence keeps its position. GOAL's optimization order is respected: this is step 1 (eliminate unnecessary work) and step 2 (unnecessary hashing), with no algorithmic change, no parallelism and no SIMD. Two audit rows are answered rather than left open. The **regression** change 0645 predicted is re-measured and disclosed: `pptx_slide_remove_boundary_save` is **+2.80%**, because its nine fingerprints per lifecycle collect zero memo hits and pay about 12,400 `Ir` of tier per part for nothing; a deck of many small parts is made worse by a design that helps a deck of large ones. The **collision margin** cost stands as designed: `2k+1` hash instances instead of one, about 9 bits of the generic 128-bit margin at `max_parts = 256`, with the three tiers domain-separated. Verdict preservation is no longer an argument but a corpus result: **78 `.pptx` fixtures, 856 packages, 9,396 ordered pairs, 0 disagreements** between `packages_equal`, the retained v1 revision and the v2 revision. No latency, RSS, allocation, throughput or cold-cache improvement is claimed. OLE2/OOXML remain active; ODF is deferred until completion and iWork excluded.

## 0655 — the memoized per-part revision proof, landed with its format bump

Change 0655 implements change 0645's design under change 0652 decision 4. `package_fingerprint` becomes `H("litchi-pptx-opened-v2" ‖ root relationships ‖ non-part members ‖ "parts" ‖ u32 count ‖ Dᵖ(p₁) ‖ …)` over per-part digests `Dᵖ(p) = H("litchi-pptx-opened-part-v2" ‖ name ‖ content type ‖ Dᵇ(payload) ‖ relationships)`, of which only the payload tier `Dᵇ(b) = H("litchi-pptx-opened-payload-v2" ‖ len ‖ b)` is memoized — keyed by `(payload address, length)`, retaining the payload `Arc` so the key cannot be recycled, and skipped entirely for any `Part` whose `blob_arc()` does not alias its `blob()`. Both durable patch families bump (`LPRM0002`, `LPCP0003`) and a patch under a recognized superseded magic is refused in `from_bytes` with the new typed `Error::DurablePatchRevisionFormat { found, expected }` before any header field is read; an unrecognized magic keeps its existing `Error::Invalid`. The `litchi_pptx::Package` facade carries the memo across opened-presentation publications, which is what makes a bare durable-patch replay cheap, and **releases** it at every mutation that publishes no snapshot to adopt, so the retention invariant holds unconditionally rather than only between publications. **Measured** by callgrind isolation pair on CPU 10: per lifecycle `pptx_eager_batch_edit_save` **−27.65%**, `pptx_eager_multi_slide_batch_edit_save` −27.53%, `pptx_slide_move_boundary_save` −7.87%, `pptx_slide_remove_boundary_save` **+2.80%**; a memoized fingerprint of the 229-part deck is 7,149,543 `Ir` against 922,730,402 cold, and the flat tier costs 13,579 `Ir` per part on that deck and 12,384 on the 25-part boundary deck. **Correctness** is gated, not argued: the pairwise oracle agrees with `packages_equal` and with the retained v1 revision on **9,396 ordered pairs over 78 repository `.pptx` fixtures** and on change 0645's synthetic 121, with zero disagreements; the alias, ABA and retention gates assert their invariants directly; the superseded-format refusal is proved by name for the forward and the inverse of both families; and every capture in the suite re-derives its revision the cold way through a `debug_assert!`. Gates: `cargo test -p litchi-pptx` **885 passed, 0 failed**, `cargo test -p litchi --features docx,xlsx,pptx,xls` **265 passed, 0 failed**, `cargo test` in `tools/perf-baseline` **540 passed, 0 failed**, plus `fmt`, Clippy, rustdoc and `non_iwork_gate.py verify`. See [Change 0655](0655-pptx-memoized-revision-proof.md); `performance_claim: none`. OLE2/OOXML remain active; ODF is deferred until completion and iWork excluded.

## 0655 — one durable format boundary moved, with a typed refusal; ADR 0005 amended for the memo

Record: [0655](0655-pptx-memoized-revision-proof.md).

**ADR 0003 is the boundary that moved, and it moved exactly as change 0645 designed.** It requires reversible, exact-source-checked patches with deterministic conflicts and no silent last-writer-wins. Redefining `package_fingerprint` changes the value both durable patch formats carry, so `LPRM0001` → `LPRM0002` and `LPCP0002` → `LPCP0003`, and a recognized superseded magic returns the new typed `Error::DurablePatchRevisionFormat { found, expected }` from `from_bytes` and `from_bytes_with_limits` — after the durable byte limit, before any header field is read, and with no package in hand — so ADR 0003's exactness is preserved by making the cross-version comparison impossible rather than by answering it wrongly. An unrecognized magic keeps today's `Error::Invalid("… has an unsupported version")`, and `DurablePatchFormat` carries no attacker-supplied bytes into a message. `litchi_pptx::Error` is `#[non_exhaustive]`, so the variant is additive. The conflict verdicts themselves are unchanged and **proved** so over the repository corpus: 9,396 ordered pairs across 78 `.pptx` fixtures plus change 0645's 121 synthetic pairs agree between `packages_equal`, the retained v1 revision and v2, with zero disagreements, and the crate's stale-source, forged-patch and drift tests pass unchanged. **ADR 0005 is amended by this record** (2026-09-16 amendment, authorized by change 0652 decision 4) to state the conditions under which an immutable snapshot, or a facade that publishes snapshots, may retain an identity-keyed digest memo: the key is an allocation the owner already holds and is retained strongly, so it cannot be recycled; every entry names an allocation the owning snapshot's own package holds and nothing is retained solely because a memo names it; a memo carried onto another package is re-projected rather than inherited; a facade adopts each publication's memo and releases it at every mutation that publishes none; a miss is an ordinary recomputation; the memo is rebuilt, never mutated; its resident cost is bounded by the part count (measured at 3,192 / 6,384 / 12,768 bytes for 29 / 85 / 221 parts, asserted under 228 bytes per entry) and charged through `try_reserve`, so exhaustion is `Error::Allocation { resource: "opened-presentation part digests" }` and not an abort; and every reused value is re-derived by a `debug_assert!` in test and debug builds. The amendment weakens no eviction rule and permits no ambient process-wide state. **ADR 0006 is untouched**: no published byte, archive layout or physical revision changes, and `physical_package_fingerprint`'s `litchi-pptx-cross-physical-v2` domain (change 0598) is unchanged — only three of `LPCP`'s six revisions are semantic. **ADR 0013 is untouched**: `notes::load_snapshot` still runs inside every capture, before the revision is taken, and the memo sits strictly inside the hash, below every structural, limit and topology check. No new `unsafe`, no new dependency, no weakened limit or malformed-input defence, no hidden global pool, no ambient I/O, no public leakage of archive types, locks or executors; `PartDigests` is `pub(crate)`. The one compliance cost stated rather than elided: the tiering offers `2k+1` SHA-256 instances to collide instead of one — about 9 bits of the 128-bit generic margin at `max_parts = 256` — in a proof ADR 0003 relies on for authorization, with the three tiers domain-separated so no digest of one tier can be read as another. `performance_claim: none`. OLE2/OOXML remain active; ODF is deferred until completion and iWork excluded.
