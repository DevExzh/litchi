# 0542 shared XLSX worksheet traversal

The user prioritizes OLE2 and OOXML performance; ODF work is deferred until that
optimization goal completes. This batch follows the measured XLSX validation
and parser attribution in 0540 and the public first-error guards in 0541.

The candidate may share a reader only for eligible original worksheet bytes.
Validation and raw parsing keep independent semantic states, complete validation
retains error precedence, and the established preprocessing and parsing path
remains authoritative for ineligible inputs. Runtime admission requires an
explicit bound on provisional parser state, independent of measured memory.

`plan.json` freezes the native primary and guard matrices, ABBA repeat order,
and admission thresholds. `allocation-gates.json` separately freezes ordinary
planning byte and incremental-peak limits. Allocation-instrumented latency is
not interpreted. The standalone refusal fixture uses a distinct fixed grid;
its shape labels do not imply byte identity with the main CRUD corpus.

All builds and children run serially under the root coordinator. Source
manifests bind tracked inputs and untracked Rust sources. Exact candidate
owners and harness additions are copied into each stage; binary copies and
receipts bind each capture to the corresponding build. The retained baseline
repeat runs under the separately bound candidate checkout. Failed attempts
must remain identified and retained, with no overwritten receipts.

The standalone guard measures `edit_sheets` only, with its result alive at the
end of the timer and allocation region. Fixture construction, source opening,
selectors, result inspection, retries, and no-op commits are outside the
region. Region peaks are observer-ordered System allocator evidence, not RSS
or a proof of adversarial safety. Native invalid latency and allocator peak
envelopes compare against the same shape's valid baseline planning operation.

Every adverse or repeat-drift flag greater than five percent requires an
individual disposition. Passing the native and allocation pilot is necessary
but insufficient for retention: planning instruction profiles, eager read
controls, semantic review, and final quality checks are also required. A
rejected implementation is restored exactly; the guard harness and evidence
may remain as a measured prerequisite for subsequent work.

The final verifier must replay analyses without changing this bundle, check
receipt and source custody, and verify quality and cleanup. The owned target
tree is removed only after its processes have finished and retained evidence
has been verified. Sealing occurs after every bundle writer has stopped.
