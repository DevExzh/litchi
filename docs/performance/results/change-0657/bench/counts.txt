=== change 0657 - deterministic instruction counts (valgrind callgrind, Ir) ===

Method: whole-process Ir at --samples 2 and --samples 42 with --warmup 0;
        Ir/op = (T42 - T2) / 40. Process start, corpus construction and all
        one-time setup cancel exactly in the difference. One 'op' is one
        harness sample iteration, which also carries the harness's own
        per-sample verification outside its timer, so Ir/op is an upper
        bound on the timed operation. taskset -c 12; callgrind with
        --separate-callers=1 --cache-sim=no --branch-sim=no.

grew by about 63,000 Ir/op over the same interval (+0.34%), so the two
roughly cancel and the whole-operation count lands at +0.038%.
