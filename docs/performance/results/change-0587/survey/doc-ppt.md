# Survey: litchi-doc and litchi-ppt (HEAD 2fc5fc657, tree read-only)

Evidence: retained 0584 profiles (`docs/performance/results/change-0584/callgrind-docppt/`), plus fresh callgrind isolation pairs (s10/s110, pinned, scratch build with the default release profile the 0584 harness also used) and native `perf stat` runs. Raw outputs: `$SCRATCH/agents/doc-ppt/` (`an-*.txt` per pair, `perf-stat-native.txt`, `survey-doc.tsv`, `survey-ppt.tsv`, `ppt-tree.txt`, driver in `docppt-survey/`). Callgrind `.out` files were deleted after extraction; the 557 MB scratch `target/` is kept.

## Why DOC open cost is not size-driven (0584's open question)

The 0584 driver measured the eager facade (`litchi_doc::Package::open` + `document()`), i.e. `Document::from_ole_with_options` (`crates/litchi-doc/src/document/package.rs:68-330`). Its cost is the sum of three terms with different drivers, and the fixtures differ in which dominates (fresh pairs, Ir per open):

| fixture | bytes | WordDocument | text units | paragraphs | Ir/open | top terms |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| saved-by-table (docsmall) | 65,024 | 39,996 | 27,250 | 443 | 5,582,903 | `TextExtractor::new` 42.9%, `PapBinTable::parse` 37.7%, `ChpBinTable::parse` 8.1% |
| FloatingPictures (docmid) | 335,360 | 38,775 | 8,915 | 210 | 3,467,311 | PAP 28.7%, text 23.0%, CHP 16.6% |
| seat-booking form (docbig) | 1,619,457 | 697,827 (+893,499 table) | 1,522 | 148 | 4,971,503 | `open_stream` zero-fill 35.4% (memset 32.8%), PAP 22.4%, **`FileInformationBlock::parse` 14.05%**, CHP 12.9% |

Term 1 is proportional to text units (docsmall has 18x docbig's text); term 2 to PAPX/CHPX entries; term 3 to stream bytes (zero-fill + copy, plus a second full copy of `WordDocument` made by the FIB parser). The 65 KB fixture costs more because it has 3x the paragraphs and 18x the text of the 1.6 MB one, whose bytes are mostly a 893 KB table stream and pictures. Drivers per fixture: `survey-doc.tsv` (columns text_units, paragraphs, pieces, chpx/papx FKP pages).

## 1. Path map

**DOC eager open** (`document/package.rs:68-330`; harness `doc_semantic_open` uses exactly this, `tools/perf-baseline/src/lib.rs:25911-25916`). Reads and retains `WordDocument`, the table stream and `Data` in full through `OleFile::open_stream` (zero-filled then copied: 0584 candidate 5). `FileInformationBlock::parse_at` copies the entire `WordDocument` suffix into the FIB (`parts/fib.rs:107`, `let data = data.to_vec();`) although the comment claims it "owns only the suffix"; the glossary FIB (`AttachedGlossary`) copies again. `TextExtractor::new` (`parts/text.rs:45-56`) decodes every piece into an unsized `Vec<u16>` with one `extend_from_slice` per ANSI character (`text.rs:359-420`; 10,722 memcpy calls per open on docsmall), then `from_utf16_lossy`, then `encode_utf16().count()`, then an 8-bytes-per-code-unit `cp_to_byte` table (`text.rs:117-125`). Then ~45 tables are parsed eagerly (`package.rs:156-233`), then `ChpBinTable::parse` and `PapBinTable::parse` walk every FKP page and resolve full properties per entry (`parts/chp_bin_table.rs:54-172`, `parts/pap_bin_table.rs:57-140`). Per PAPX entry: `[sprms, piece_modifier].concat()` (`pap_bin_table.rs:218`), a `HashSet` for indirection cycle checks (`:196`), a throwaway `parse_sprms` scan for huge-PAPX opcodes when there is no Data stream (`:197-208`), a baseline clone (`pap/parser.rs:200-215`), `apply_direct_sprms` parsing the sprms again (`:175`), and `from_sprm_with_stylesheet(direct_sprms)` re-resolving the whole paragraph a second time only to copy its table state (`:186-195`). The single-entry adjacent style cache (`pap_bin_table.rs:222-236`, change 0051) misses whenever consecutive paragraphs alternate styles: 103 baseline re-resolutions for 445 entries on docsmall. Measured per open on docsmall: `parse_sprms` 1,931 calls (about 3 per entry), `ParagraphProperties::clone` 548, `concat` 626, `malloc` 4,524. `Document::text()` clones the whole string per call (`document/model/semantic/basics.rs:19-20`); `paragraph_at` builds a `ParagraphExtractor` per subdocument range but resolves through the 0053/0056 indexes (`layout.rs:8-36`). MTEF extraction is compiled out without the `formula` feature (`document/codec.rs:45-49`); the `litchi` facade does not enable it (`crates/litchi/Cargo.toml:49`).

**DOC owned editor** (`body_text::Snapshot`, used by every `doc_semantic_*_edit_save` and `doc_body_snapshot_*` selector, `lib.rs:26003-26010`): `open_bounded` (`body_text.rs:570-590`) runs the strict owner parse and then `Package::validate_ole_file` (`package/codec.rs:96-99`), which executes the complete eager `Document` parse and discards it; `commit` (`body_text.rs:1651-1660`) reopens the candidate the same way. Every eager-open cost above is therefore paid twice per edit (0160 attribution; 0161 rejected borrowing it).

**DOC source-backed snapshot** (`body_text::source::SourceSnapshot`, change 0105): `open_with_options` (`body_text/source.rs:499-538`) opens the CFB index twice (`:510`, `:532`) and calls `identity_fingerprint` three times (`:526`, `:591`, `:599`); each call is `plan_same_length_stream_splices(Vec::new())` (`:2089-2095`, `crates/litchi-cfb/src/splice.rs:179-190`), whose `overlay::fingerprints` (`crates/litchi-cfb/src/overlay.rs:1320-1344`) streams the whole artifact through **two** SHA-256 hashers even though an empty splice makes source and target identical. `ensure_current` hashes twice more (`source.rs:766`, `:774`) and `write_validated` twice again (`overlay.rs:913-940`). The semantic work (FIB prefix, DOP prefix, CLX) is a few KB of ranged reads.

**PPT source-backed presentation** (`SourceBackedPackage::from_path` + `presentation()`, `crates/litchi-ppt/src/presentation/package.rs:167-300`; harness `ppt_semantic_*`, `ppt_pictures_source_backed_*`): reads `PowerPoint Document` and `Current User` in full (`:214-218`, zero-filled), parses the complete record tree leniently (`parsers/parser.rs:51-86`), where `Record::parse_impl` copies every record payload into an owned `Vec<u8>` and then parses children from the same slice, so container bytes are copied once per nesting level (`records/record.rs:175-200`). Measured copy factor (owned payload bytes / stream bytes): 1.63 on 45543.ppt (286 records, depth 5), 1.32 on the LibreOffice resave, 2.01 on SampleShow.ppt (`ppt-tree.txt`); the retained `Presentation` keeps the stream too (`presentation/model.rs:133-148`), so retention is about 2.3-3x the stream. `parse_document_with_limits` ends with `extract_slide_text_from_document` (`parser.rs:83`, `:108-118`), an eager full-text extraction stored in `slide_atoms_sets`, which nothing in `presentation/` consumes (the 8 `self.parser` uses are `find_records_ref`). `inspect_live_mapping` (`embedded/object/editor/lifecycle/open.rs:41-53`) and `SlideDirectory::build_with_limits` (`slide/directory.rs:94`) walk persist directories only. Pictures are lazy (0116/0117). Fresh pair on 45543.ppt (385,024 B): 1,181,516 Ir/open; `parse_impl` 47.2%, memcpy 30.4% (877 calls), `open_stream` 30.0% (memset 27.6%), `SlideDirectory` 7.9%, `extract_text` 259 calls.

**PPT text editor**: the owned `text_edit::Snapshot::from_bytes` (`text_edit.rs:221-224`) runs a full `presentation()` parse as validation; `commit` (`:1837-1890`) reopens an `Editor` over a copy of the bytes, replaces one persisted record, `finish()`es a whole CFB, and re-validates with another full parse plus readback. The source-backed `text_edit::SourceSnapshot::open` (`:399-449`) does no semantic work at all: it is one `plan_splices(Vec::new())` (`:423`), i.e. two SHA-256 passes over the artifact; `publish_source_operation` (`:944-1000`) hashes at least four more times.

**Writers**: `litchi-doc` and `litchi-ppt` `writer/core` paths are fresh-creation serializers (`writer/core/package/package.rs:107-175`, `writer/core/package.rs:248-270`): they rebuild every stream and a new CFB; there is no unchanged-stream passthrough in them, and they are not the edit path. The edit paths (owned editors above) re-render the whole CFB once per commit through `litchi-ole-common` `put_streams_shared` (0017) or `Editor::finish`; the source-backed splices (0100/0105) write the exact source bytes with spans applied.

**Locks**: `SharedOleFile` carries Mutex/RwLock-guarded state (20 mentions in `litchi-cfb/src/shared.rs`); single-threaded opens take them uncontended, not measured. **Whole-document work on a one-item read**: every eager open above; the owned editors' validation; the source-backed fingerprints.

## 2. Remaining opportunities (ranked)

**R1. Source-backed DOC/PPT opens are dominated by whole-artifact SHA-256, computed 2-6 times per open.** GOAL step 1 (unnecessary work) and 2 (copying/hashing). Mechanism: (a) `overlay::fingerprints` runs a target hasher for an empty splice, which duplicates the source hasher byte for byte; compute once and copy when `spans` is empty; (b) `SourceSnapshot::open` computes the identity three times (`source.rs:526`, `:591`, `:599`) over the same immutable index; the third repeats the second's read-twice-compare defence; (c) the `ReadAt` path parses the CFB index twice (`:510`, `:532`), which `open_owned_source` already avoids. Evidence: fresh pairs `an-docsnap-dup.txt` (64 KB: 41,107,039 Ir/open, `identity_fingerprint` 99.6%, `sha2` compress 98.2%), `an-docsnap-pic.txt` (1.4 MB: 912,551,293 Ir/open, 99.2% SHA-256), `an-pptmid-textedit.txt` (385 KB: 81,168,004 Ir/open, `sha2` 98.8%, versus 1,181,516 for the full eager presentation). Native `perf stat` (SHA-NI active, so callgrind overstates by ~19x): PPT text-edit open 3.31 M cycles/op vs 0.25 M for the eager open (13.4x); DOC snapshot open on picture.doc 36.9 M cycles/op; like-for-like on fixtures both paths accept, snapshot vs eager: table-merged-cells.doc 660,581 vs 266,357 cycles (2.5x), noheadfoot-litchi.doc 486,617 vs 74,951 (6.5x) (`perf-stat-native.txt`). Record status: 0105/0100/0119 designed the "complete artifact fingerprint"; 0143 coalesced its reads; 0165 made the owned editor's FNV fingerprint lazy (precedent); 0586's zero result is explained by this term (a read hint cannot register against 41 M Ir of hashing). No record prices the CPU cost; none found by `grep -ril 'sha-ni|fingerprint cost'`. Size: measured (above); a one-hasher empty-splice plan halves every identity pass; dropping the third DOC pass and the second index open removes a further third. Scenarios: Read/snapshot and Opened-document CRUD for DOC source-backed paragraph read/edit (no registered selector), PPT `ppt_source_backed_fresh_open_one_shape_text`, `ppt_source_backed_one_shape_text`, `ppt_source_backed_repeated_shape_text`, and every source-backed commit. Constraints: ADR 0006 source-identity fence and 0582's differential fuzz must be re-run; this is a fence redesign, so a frozen design record is required, and (b) may need an ADR 0006 note. Risk: medium. Falsified if: native cycles per `SourceSnapshot::open` do not fall by at least the removed passes' share, or the fence fuzz finds a stable-token mutation the reduced fence misses.

**R2. DOC text extraction does four passes and one memcpy per character.** Step 2. Mechanism: size `text` from `total_cps()`; decode Windows-1252 through a 256-entry `u16` table with `push` instead of `encode_utf16` + `extend_from_slice` per byte (`text.rs:399-405`); drop `encode_utf16().count()` (it equals `utf16.len()`); build `cp_to_byte` in the same pass as the UTF-16 decode, or lazily (it is 8 bytes per code unit, 218 KB for docsmall). Evidence: docsmall 2,396,879 Ir/open in `TextExtractor::new` (42.9%), memcpy self 1,042,009 Ir with 10,722 calls, `Map::fold` 14.5%, realloc growth 8.7% (`an-docsmall-facade.txt`). Record status: none found (only raw perf dumps mention `TextExtractor`; 0050 indexes pieces, not decoding). Size: measured; bounded by ~40% of small-document opens, less on picture-heavy files. Scenarios: `doc_semantic_open`, `doc_semantic_full_text`, `doc_semantic_paragraph_count`, and both validation passes of every `doc_semantic_*_edit_save`. Constraints: text must stay byte-identical (lossy surrogate handling at `text.rs:98-105`). Risk: low; no ADR. Falsified if: docsmall open Ir falls by less than 10%.

**R3. PAPX entries are resolved about three times each.** Step 1. Mechanism: parse each grpprl once and pass the `Vec<Sprm>` to the huge-PAPX check, `apply_direct_sprms` and the table-state derivation (`pap/parser.rs:186` re-runs a full cascade per paragraph only to copy five table fields); replace the single-entry baseline cache with a small map keyed by `istd`; reuse one `HashSet` and one concat buffer across entries. Evidence: docsmall `PapBinTable::parse` 2,106,737 Ir/open (37.7%), docbig 1,114,729 (22.4%), docmid 28.7%; `parse_sprms` 1,931 calls for ~626 entries, `paragraph_style_on_baseline` 103 calls for 17 FKP pages, clone 548, `concat` 626 per open. Record status: 0051 landed the single-entry cache and names adjacent repetition only; 0056 indexes containment for queries; nothing covers the double cascade or the sprm re-parse. Size: measured upper bound is the PAP share; modelled saving one half to two thirds of it. Scenarios: as R2 plus `doc_semantic_one_paragraph(_at)`, `doc_semantic_list_paragraphs`. Constraints: property semantics locked by `parts/pap/tests.rs` and `pap_bin_table.rs` tests; the 0051 cache re-key rule must be preserved. Risk: low-medium; no ADR. Falsified if: `parse_sprms` calls per entry do not drop to one or docsmall open falls by less than 10%.

**R4. The FIB owns a copy of the whole `WordDocument` stream.** Step 2. Mechanism: `parse_at` should copy only the FIB's declared extent (FibBase + csw + cslw + cbRgFcLcb + cswNew, about 1.3 KB) or keep an offset view; audit `fib.data` readers for accesses past that extent. Evidence: docbig `FileInformationBlock::parse_at` 698,295 Ir/open = one copy of the 697,827-byte stream (14.05%); a second copy occurs for glossary FIBs. Record status: none found (`grep -ril FileInformationBlock docs/performance` hits only raw dumps). Size: measured on docbig; proportional to `WordDocument` length on every DOC open and both owned-editor validations. Risk: low; no ADR. Falsified if: docbig memcpy bytes do not fall by ~700 KB per open.

**R5. PPT record tree copies every byte once per nesting level and retains the stream as well.** Step 2/3. Mechanism: let container records not own their payload (children already own the bytes), or make `Record.data` a range into the retained `powerpoint_document`; `Record.data` is a public field (`record.rs:29`), so this is an API change inside litchi-ppt. Evidence: copy factors 1.32-2.01 (`ppt-tree.txt`); memcpy 30.4% and allocation ~15% of the 45543.ppt open; `finish_grow` 11.1%. Record status: none for PPT; 0199/0200 did the analogous elision for ODS parse events; 0301 explicitly leaves "the complete Document ... parser" outside its scope. Size: measured copy factor; Ir saving modelled at up to the container share of memcpy (about 40% of copied bytes on 45543.ppt). Scenarios: `ppt_semantic_open`, `ppt_semantic_list_slides`, `ppt_semantic_full_text`, `ppt_semantic_*_edit_save` (owned validation parses twice), `ppt_pictures_*_open`. Risk: medium (public type, editors read `record.data`). Falsified if: memcpy per open does not fall by at least the container payload share.

**R6. Eager full-text extraction inside `parse_document_with_limits`.** Step 1. `extract_slide_text_from_document` (`parser.rs:83`, `:108-118`) builds the whole presentation text at every open into `slide_atoms_sets`, consumed only by `RecordParser::slides()`/`slide_count()`, which no crate outside litchi-ppt calls. Evidence: 259 `extract_text` calls per open; ~2.7% of the retained pptmid profile. Record status: none found. Size: measured, small. Risk: low; the `RecordParser` API is public, so a deprecation note is needed. Falsified if: any consumer of `RecordParser::slides()` exists.

**R7. Deferred, priced, recorded: stream zero-fill on open** (memset 32.8% docbig, 27.6% pptmid, sites `OleFile::read_stream_from_fat` and `SharedOleFile::read_fat_stream`) is 0584 candidate 5 / 0574 opportunity 4 and stays blocked on GOAL rule 10 until the reader API appends into the buffer; nothing new here except that DOC and PPT are the largest payers.

## 3. Looks like an opportunity but is not

- Lazy parsing of the ~45 DOC tables or of the FKPs on a one-paragraph read: the eager `Document` is the documented general reader (42/57 fixtures) and doubles as the mandatory public-reader gate (`validate_ole_file`, 0160); ADR 0005 mandatory validation and 0574 opportunity 6's rejection apply; a lazy PAP/CHP index would be the DOC analogue of 0576's frozen SST design and needs its own record.
- Borrowing or skipping the owned editor's second validation: 0161 rejected (both directions regressed).
- Hinting `resolve_paragraph`'s ranged reads: 0586 measured zero on every fixture and reverted; R1 explains why.
- Routing the facade to `SourceSnapshot`: it refuses 49/57 fixtures by design (0105's refusal boundary), so it is not a general reader; and today it is slower than the eager open (R1).
- `collect_exact`, `directory_name_data`, collector variants in the CFB index: 0524/0548/0549/0554/0533 rejected.
- PPT `Pictures` laziness and selected-slide decoding: landed (0116/0117, 0301); `Presentation::text` already streams per slide.
- Bounded parallelism inside a DOC/PPT open: opens cost 1-6 M Ir; GOAL rule 9 and the 1/2/4/8/12 evidence make it a loss.

## 4. Measurement blockers

- **SourceSnapshot refusals (49/57 .doc):** Drawing 34, Field 4, AmbiguousTopology 4, Encrypted 3, Revision 2, Macro 1, plus one CFB error (`redline-1.doc`, a deliberate fail fixture). These are typed refusals from `reject_fib_policy`/`reject_container_components` (`source.rs:1417-1522`), correct under 0105's contract, not parser gaps; only 8 fixtures (largest `picture.doc`, 1.4 MB; the rest ≤ 64 KB) can drive source-backed DOC scenarios, and no registered selector opens one.
- **Facade refusals (15/57):** 5 `style names and aliases must be unique` (`duplicate-style-names`, `footnote`, `lists-margins`, `picture`, `pictures_escher`; `StyleSheet::parse_with_leniency` and `OpenOptions.leniency` exist at `package.rs:228-230`, unverified whether they admit these), 3 `PasswordRequired`, 4 Word 6/95 (unsupported by design), 2 field-table consistency refusals on ordinary Word files (`watermark.doc`: `grffldEnd.fNested`; POI `test.doc`: `fHasSep`) that merit a correctness check under ADR 0006, 1 byte order. No fixture above 335 KB is accepted by both readers, so the two DOC paths cannot be compared on a large file.
- **PPT:** 26/30 open; 4 are CryptoAPI-encrypted; `text_edit::SourceSnapshot::open` accepts all 30 because it parses nothing.
- **Callgrind mis-prices R1:** under valgrind `sha2` uses `sha256::soft` (81.2 M Ir) while natively SHA-NI is active (4.30 M instructions, 3.31 M cycles); R1 must be priced in cycles.
- The 0584 and this survey's drivers use throwaway operation boundaries; no allocation, RSS or cold-cache numbers exist for DOC/PPT; the record-tree copy factor is computed from the tree, not measured RSS.
- Defects noticed, not fixed: `fib.rs:71-75` doc comment contradicts `:107`; `presentation/model.rs:137` marks `parser` `dead_code` although eight methods use it; `parser.rs:112` "treat all text as a single slide" leaves `RecordParser::slide_count()` semantically wrong.
