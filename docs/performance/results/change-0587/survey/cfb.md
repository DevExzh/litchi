# Survey: litchi-cfb (CFB/OLE2 substrate) — remaining opportunities at HEAD 2fc5fc657

Scratch evidence: `$SCRATCH/agents/cfb/` — `cfb_open_model.py` + `cfb_open_model.txt` (byte-layout census of the OLE2 corpus and the eight 0584 fixtures), `strace-*.txt` + `strace-summary.txt` (syscalls per open in `file-source` mode), `docppt-fixtures.txt`. Retained profile used throughout: `docs/performance/results/change-0584/` (ranking.txt, analysis/memcpy-memset-callers.txt, callgrind-xls/tree-head-flagship-one-cell-s220.txt).

## 1. Path map

**Open (one path for both readers).** `SharedOleFile::open_with_limits` (shared.rs:587-644) observes `version()` three times and `len()` once, runs the eager parser `OleFile::open_with_limits` over a `ReadAtCursor` adapter (shared.rs:3157-3205; every `read_exact` is one `read_at`), then moves the tables out with `into_parsed_index` (no copy). The eager parser (file.rs:589-771): header 512 B; DIFAT sectors one at a time into a stack buffer (file.rs:832-835; zero fixtures declare DIFAT); FAT read in contiguous runs (0570) through a zero-allocated `sector_batch_scratch` (64 KiB cap, file.rs:1660-1684) into `fat: Vec<u32>` — the whole FAT is materialized (file/128 bytes); directory chain read into a zero-filled `dir_data` (file.rs:1025-1026) and parsed **twice**: `validated_directory_entries` (red-black sibling order, SID ownership, name folding; one transient `String` + `SmallVec` pair per entry, file.rs:1230-1358) then `parse_directory_entry` per reachable SID via `build_storage_tree_iterative` (a second `String` name; `format_clsid` allocates only for non-zero CLSIDs, file.rs:3068-3076; `children` is never populated at open), with a third loop reconciling both passes (file.rs:1053-1076). MiniFAT: exact chain, run-batched into `minifat: Vec<u32>` (file.rs:953-995). Then the two whole-container validations: `validate_stream_allocations` walks **every stream's full chain** with `SectorChainScratch::collect_exact` and claims every sector into `sector_roles: Vec<PhysicalSectorRole>` (1 byte per physical sector) plus a mini-sector bitset (file.rs:1110-1207); `validate_physical_sector_layout` iterates every physical sector (file.rs:1209-1224). Work proportional to the whole document, not the touched closure: FAT (file/128 B), `sector_roles` (file/512 B), the chain walk of every stream, the physical-layout loop. All four are ADR 0005 "mandatory structural validation" (0574 opp 6 rejected making them closure-proportional; GOAL rule 12). Incidental: the second directory parse and its per-entry `String`s (needed by the public `DirectoryEntry`/`directory_entries()` API and ADR 0026 consumers). Measured shares on the flagship open (0584 ranking.txt): `collect_exact` 3.94%, `validate_physical_sector_layout` 1.38%, `validate_stream_allocations` 1.31%, `directory_name_data` 1.02%.

**Read (shared).** `open_stream` (shared.rs:880-987): `find_entry` (fold the caller's name with `directory_name_data`, walk the sibling tree; shared.rs:2637-2680) then, for FAT streams, `read_fat_stream` = **zero-fill `size` bytes** (shared.rs:2427) + `read_chain_into` → `read_sector_run` → `read_exact_at(pos, &mut out[..present])` (shared.rs:2538-2651), one trailing fence. MiniFAT streams ≤4095 B with the root-size ratio gate are read as exact ranges (direct mode, single-flight atomics, 0146-0152); otherwise the root ministream is materialized by `read_fat_stream` and copied again by `Vec::into` → `Arc<[u8]>` (shared.rs:2528-2536), then copied mini-sector by mini-sector into a third zero-filled buffer (shared.rs:2480-2523). `read_stream_range_hinted` (shared.rs:1024-1154): `find_entry` per call, full-stream fast path via `read_chain_into`, otherwise a hint-resumed walk (0579) and physical runs read straight into the caller's buffer (no cfb zero-fill), one fence (0558). `stream_cursor_at_hinted` (shared.rs:762-846) resumes the construction walk (0585); the cursor coalesces contiguous sectors per `read_exact` (shared.rs:2861-2896) and fences once per call (2807-2836); its MiniFAT `physical_span` indexes `root_chain` in O(1) (2346-2388). Locks: the `ministream` `Mutex` only on the cache path; FAT reads take none. Budget: single reads charge nothing; only the bulk session charges `Resource::Work` (total bytes) and reserves `Resource::Memory` per batch (shared_bulk.rs:133-146). On a `FileSource`, `version()` and `len()` are `metadata()` calls (litchi-core/src/source/file.rs:128,152) — **measured** in `file-source` mode: 34 statx + 53 pread64 per flagship open, 47 + 61 per flagship one-cell, 30 + 40 per 54016 open (21 ops each; `strace-summary.txt`). The read-ahead layer absorbs the ~2,099 logical range reads per flagship open (465,978 calls / 222 ops in tree-head-flagship-one-cell-s220.txt).

**Zero-fill sites (production).** (a) file.rs:1025 `dir_data` — fully overwritten except a truncated-tail sector; (b) file.rs:1644 `read_sector_into`: `buffer.fill(0)` then `read_exact(&mut buffer[..present])` — only `[present..]` needs zero; (c) file.rs:1684 `sector_batch_scratch` zero-allocated, and `read_sector_run_into` re-zeroes each run (file.rs:1746) — double zero, tail-only needed; (d) file.rs:2133 `read_stream_from_fat`: `try_filled_vec(size, 0)` then `read_sectors_batched` overwrites `[..present]` per run — **the DOC site**; (e) shared.rs:2427 `read_fat_stream` — same shape, **the PPT site**, also feeding `load_ministream`; (f) shared.rs:2146/2480 MiniFAT copies — fully overwritten by `copy_from_slice`; (g) shared.rs:3141 `clone_minifat_waiter_payload` — zero then copy; (h) litchi-xls `source.rs:1641` (0574 opp 4, not cfb). The eager `read_stream_from_fat` additionally allocates per call a FAT-sized zeroed `CheckedBitSet` and a `Vec<u32>` of the whole chain and walks to ENDOFCHAIN regardless of declared size (`collect_sector_chain`, file.rs:2774-2812), duplicating the open-time validation that `read_chain_into` relies on instead. **Census (modelled, `cfb_open_model.txt`):** memset Ir per open equals the bytes of the streams slurped whole — docbig 1,595,476 Ir vs 1,595,422 B (WordDocument+1Table+Data, 98.5% of the file); pptmid 315,656 vs 315,620 B; docmid 315,712 vs 315,658 B. Median slurped/size across the corpus: xls 48.7% (source-backed XLS does not slurp; its memset is the xls site), doc 57.2%, ppt 53.4%.

**Eager vs shared users.** Eager `OleFile<R: Read+Seek>` (`&mut self` reads, whole-stream `Vec` results): all DOC package opens (litchi-doc/src/package/codec.rs:89; document/package.rs:84,140,143 slurp WordDocument, Table, Data), DOC embedded objects/MTEF, PPT `Package` (litchi-ppt/src/package/codec.rs:90), XLS pivot_editor.rs:330, sheet_visibility.rs:1182-1183/1465/1490, vba.rs, xml_map, every ole-common property-set/custom-XML/object-directory/protection reader (`&OleFile<R>`), and the writer's and overlay's own output re-parse (writer/sequential.rs:970, overlay.rs:1305). Shared `SharedOleFile`: XLS source-backed (raw.rs:22-26), PPT source-backed (package/source.rs:91) and `presentation/package.rs:214-216` (whole-stream `open_stream`), DOC `body_text::source` (refuses 49/57 fixtures, 0584), validation entry points. `SharedOleBulkRead` has **no production caller outside litchi-cfb**.

**Rayon session** (shared_bulk.rs:105-228): builds its own `rayon::ThreadPool` sized by `context.limits().workers()`, never the global pool; parallel only when workers > 1, batch.len() > 1 and batch bytes ≥ `min_parallel_bytes`; batches bounded by `max_in_flight_tasks/bytes`; budget charged before payload reads; cancellation checked per batch. Governed by `ExecutionContext` as Workstream F asks — and unused.

**Writer/save.** `OleWriter` (writer/core.rs:250-1222) is a from-scratch builder: every save re-serializes header, FAT, DIFAT, MiniFAT, directory and every stream, and the format crates feed it by `open_stream` → `create_stream` copies of every unchanged stream (litchi-xls/src/writer/core/package.rs, litchi-doc/src/writer/core/package/package.rs, litchi-ppt/src/writer/core/package.rs), retaining all stream bytes until `write_to`. Same-length edits use `ValidatedOverlayPlan` (overlay.rs:651-870): plan = two SHA-256 passes over the whole source (`fingerprints`, 1321-1345) + `SharedOleFile::open` of the composed view (1029) + eager reopen with full per-selection compares (1305-1316); publish = full streaming copy with spans applied plus two more SHA-256 passes for generic sources (919-937; 0175 dropped them for owned-immutable sources). There is no sector-level copy-through for length-changing edits.

## 2. Remaining opportunities (ranked)

**1. Append-style stream reads so the whole-stream zero-fill never exists (GOAL step 2).** Mechanism: sites (d) and (e) memset `size` bytes that `read_sectors_batched`/`read_sector_run` overwrite in full except the truncated-final-sector tail. Safe shape without `unsafe`: a provided `ReadAt` method `read_to_vec_at(offset, len, &mut Vec<u8>)` (litchi-core/src/source.rs:62-100) whose default keeps today's zero-fill and which `OwnedSource`/`SliceSource`/`OwnedArcSource` override with `extend_from_slice`; cfb gains an appending `read_chain_into` that pushes each physical run and zero-extends only the missing tail, keeping 0570's typed refusal for a run starting at/after EOF. `FileSource` cannot avoid it on stable Rust (`FileExt::read_at` needs an initialized slice; `BorrowedBuf` is unstable), so on real files only the tail trim (item 4) applies. Evidence: memset Ir == slurped bytes (above); callers `OleFile::read_stream_from_fat` 32.15%/9.13%/0.98% of docbig/docmid/docsmall open Ir, `SharedOleFile::read_fat_stream` 21.6%/26.7% of pptbig/pptmid (memcpy-memset-callers.txt). Record status: 0574 opp 4 (xls site only, "needs MaybeUninit"), 0584 cand 5 (names the cfb sites, blocked on GOAL rule 10 and API shape), 0570 (silent zero-fill warning); no record designs the safe shape. Size: measured Ir upper bound 9-32% of DOC/PPT opens (`rep stosb` per byte overstates); cycles unknown; realistic effect on in-memory sources is memset+memcpy → memcpy on the slurp, none on `FileSource`. Scenarios: Read/snapshot (DOC open/text, PPT open, PPT presentation open), any `open_stream` consumer; no registered selector (0584's throwaway driver). Constraints: GOAL rules 10, 11 (litchi-core trait addition), 12 (`try_reserve` before append); ADR 0005 untouched; tests `fat_stream_read_zero_fills_a_truncated_final_sector`, `batched_fat_reads_zero_fill_a_truncated_final_sector`, `direct_open_zero_fills_a_truncated_final_root_sector_like_the_cache` are the parity gate. Risk medium; frozen design record required, no ADR. Falsified if: a perf-stat cycles A/B of docbig/pptmid open on an owned source is inside the 4% p50 floor — plausible, since a 1.6 MB memset is ~60-80 µs on this host against an open of roughly 1-2 ms.

**2. Copy-through writer for length-changing OLE2 saves (GOAL steps 1-2; "LEGACY CFB-SPECIFIC WORK").** Today every OLE2 edit-save decodes every unchanged stream (zero-fill + copy), retains all of them, and re-serializes every sector; the overlay path covers same-length edits only. A copy-through writer would take a validated `SharedOleFile`, stream untouched streams' sectors verbatim (as the overlay already does), and rebuild only FAT/DIFAT/MiniFAT/directory plus the changed streams' sectors (appended after the last used sector or placed in FREESECT runs). Invariants it must prove: every physical sector claimed once and every unclaimed sector FREESECT (`validate_physical_sector_layout`), FAT/DIFAT counts and FATSECT/DIFSECT markers, directory red-black order/colour and SID ownership (ADR 0026 binding), v3 size masking, mini-stream cutoff migration (a mini stream growing past 4096 moves to the FAT and the root chain stays exact), byte-identical untouched streams (GOAL rule 2), deterministic output, and reopen through the normal parser before a sink observes a byte (the 0175/overlay policy). Nothing in ADR 0005/0006 or GOAL forbids it; whether FREESECT reuse vs append is preservation-neutral is an open policy question. Record status: 0387/0420 (copy-on-write) are OPC; 0103/0142/0143/0172/0175 are same-length; 0003/0036 (OleWriter) do not cover it — none found for CFB length-changing copy-through. Size: unknown — no OLE2 length-changing save selector exists; modelled: peak memory O(file) → O(bounded buffer) and one fewer decode+zero-fill per unchanged stream; bytes written stay O(file) (atomic publication). Scenarios: Opened-document CRUD saves in xls/doc/ppt. Risk high; frozen design record plus an ADR clarification of physical-layout policy first. Falsified if: a phase attribution of one xls/doc/ppt save shows the crates' own record re-encoding, not the container copy, dominates.

**3. Bounded chain walk in the eager stream readers (GOAL step 2).** `read_stream_from_fat`/`read_stream_from_minifat` (file.rs:2115-2131, 2207) call `collect_sector_chain`, allocating a FAT-sized zeroed bitset and a full-chain `Vec<u32>` and walking to ENDOFCHAIN per `open_stream`; the shared `read_chain_into` walks exactly `required` sectors with no allocation and refuses short/long chains. Port that shape. Record status: 0190/0413/0511 cover the open-time scratch; grep of `read_stream_from_fat` finds no record on this per-read path; 0293 covers ranges only. Size: modelled — ≤2,738 FAT entries on this corpus (bitset 344 B, Vec ≤11 KiB), i.e. below noise; a 1 GiB v3 file would pay a 256 KiB memset + 8 MiB Vec per `open_stream`. Scenarios: every eager consumer (DOC open, ole-common property sets, PPT `Package`). Risk low; no ADR; needs a synthetic large fixture to measure. Falsified if: no large-file fixture is admitted.

**4. Tail-only zeroing in the sector helpers (GOAL step 2).** `read_sector_into` (file.rs:1644) and `read_sector_run_into` (1746) fill the whole buffer then overwrite `[..present]`; `sector_batch_scratch` (1684) zero-allocates on top. `[present..].fill(0)` is semantics-preserving. Size: measured 0.3-0.5% of DOC/PPT open Ir (memcpy-memset-callers.txt) — a hygiene change to bundle with item 1, never to land alone. Record status: 0570. Falsified if: measured alone (it will be inside noise).

**5. Ministream cache built with two extra copies (GOAL step 2).** `load_ministream` zero-fills and reads, then `Vec::into` → `Arc<[u8]>` copies again (shared.rs:2528-2536); `read_minifat_stream` copies a third time into a zero-filled buffer. Storing `Arc<Vec<u8>>` (or building from item 1's appending read) removes one full copy. Size: modelled — ministreams are 128 B to 4,928 B on the eight primary fixtures and the direct-read policy (0146-0152) keeps the cache unmaterialized for ≤4095 B streams; effectively zero on this corpus, real only for ministream-heavy documents. Record status: 0146/0148/0149/0152; none on the Arc copy. Risk low. Falsified if: a corpus census shows no ministream above ~64 KiB.

**6. Resolved-entry handle for repeated reads (GOAL step 1).** Every `read_stream_range_hinted`, `stream_cursor_at_hinted` and `open_stream` re-folds the path and walks the sibling tree; the hint already records `sid`. Measured upper bound: `directory_name_data` self 0.47% + `find_entry` self 0.43% of flagship one-cell Ir (tree-head-flagship-one-cell-s220.txt). A public `StreamHandle` resolved once is the clean shape. Record status: 0002 (lookup), 0554 (open-time handoff rejected), 0559 (ASCII fold). Size < 1%; not worth a change alone. Falsified if: cycles put the fold inside noise (expected).

Honest floor statement: for this corpus (max 1.6 MB, ≤67 directory entries, 0 DIFAT-bearing and 0 v4 files among 126 xls / 56 doc / 30 ppt fixtures parsed) the substrate's open is at its floor — its remaining symbols are mandatory validations or thrice-rejected micro-work — and the only cfb-attributable cost above noise is the whole-stream zero-fill on the DOC/PPT slurp paths (item 1). Items 3 and 5 only become measurable with synthetic large, DIFAT-bearing or v4 corpora, which `OleWriter::with_sector_size(4096)` can generate.

## 3. Looks like an opportunity but is not

- **Closure-proportional `validate_stream_allocations`/`validate_physical_sector_layout`** (5.64%+1.38% of flagship open): rejected, 0574 opp 6 — ADR 0005 mandatory structural validation, GOAL rule 12.
- **Coalescing reads across non-contiguous runs**: rejected, 0574 opp 7 — reads sectors owned by other streams (ADR 0006 ownership boundary); reads are already at the physical-run minimum.
- **`collect_exact` micro-optimizations** (3.94%): rejected by 0524, 0548, 0549; 0533 forbids reviving 0524.
- **Single directory parse / name handoff** (7.8% on small fixtures): tried and rejected by 0554 — every primary XLS p50 regressed 2-11%; 0559 landed the ASCII fold instead. The second `DirectoryEntry` parse also backs the public API and ADR 0026 consumers.
- **Lazy CLSID formatting**: `format_clsid` already returns an empty `String` for all-zero CLSIDs (file.rs:3068-3076); only the root and storages allocate.
- **Per-read version fence as a syscall storm on file sources**: refuted by measurement — 34 statx / 53 pread64 per flagship open in `file-source` mode; 0558/0560 already single-fence, and the read-ahead layer absorbs the 2,099 logical reads.
- **Generic-source overlay publish hashing four times and parsing twice**: 0143 coalesced the reads; 0175 removed the plan/publish scans for owned-immutable sources and deliberately withheld generic/mutable sources because a `SourceVersion` fence is weaker than the `TargetFingerprintChanged` content guarantee. Changing that is an ADR 0006 policy amendment, not an optimization.
- **Parallel bulk reads for DOC's three open-time streams**: the session exists and is correctly governed, but the 1/2/4/8/12 evidence says small tasks regress and the corpus has ≤3 streams of ≤1.6 MB per open.
- **DIFAT loop** (file.rs:832): 0 of 212 parsed fixtures declare DIFAT (0574 opp 8; my census).
- **Early exit / laziness in mandatory work** (`query_cell`, rels): 0574 opp 6, 0577.

## 4. Measurement blockers

- **Corpus ceiling**: no fixture above 1.6 MB, none with DIFAT or 4096-byte sectors, ≤67 directory entries — items 3 and 5 and any large-file claim are unmeasurable on real fixtures; synthetic corpora are needed.
- **No registered OLE2 selectors for DOC/PPT opens or for any OLE2 length-changing save**; 0584's DOC/PPT numbers came from a throwaway driver (`results/change-0584/harness/`). DOC's shared path refuses 49/57 fixtures (`style names and aliases must be unique`, litchi-doc/src/parts/styles/semantic/validation.rs:204), so DOC is effectively eager-only.
- **Instrument limits**: callgrind counts `rep stosb`/`rep movsb` per byte (item 1 is an upper bound) and runs `sha2` on its `soft` backend because valgrind masks the SHA CPUID bit — the retained flagship profile's 53% `sha2::sha256::soft::unroll::compress` is the harness's per-sample identity check, and any Ir pricing of the overlay fingerprints is wrong; use `perf stat` cycles.
- **Harness note**: `xls_source_attribution --operation one-cell` defaults to worksheet index 1 and fails with `WorksheetNotFound("1")` on single-sheet fixtures such as 54016.xls unless `--worksheet-index 0` is passed (my `file-source` run of that leg exited early; the other three legs are valid).
- **No peak-RSS measurement** exists for the writer path (`OleWriter` retains every stream until `write_to`), and no production caller exercises `SharedOleBulkRead`, so an end-to-end Workstream F claim for OLE2 cannot be tested.
- **Cross-area observations (not cfb)**: the `Arc<T,A>::from_box_in` memcpy on DOC opens (4.04% docsmall, 2.06% docmid) is a `Vec`→`Arc<[u8]>` copy inside litchi-doc or the driver, not the ministream (docsmall's ministream is 128 B); `FileInformationBlock::parse_at` copies 697 KB (14% of docbig); `Record::parse_impl` copies 158-252 KB per PPT open (17-21%). No correctness defects were found in litchi-cfb on this pass.
