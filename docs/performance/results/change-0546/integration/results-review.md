## Pilot and conditional gate result

All measured pilot gates and conditional analyses pass. The four primary native shape/repeat rows improve total elapsed p50 by 7.086069%–10.796697%, total mean by 7.023813%–10.313431%, and planning p50 by 24.769115%–26.512867%. These ranges cover every required primary shape and repeat.

Ir is diagnostic attribution under its declared owner and is not converted into a latency claim. Hardware passes full coverage for the eight planned whole-child cases and remains diagnostic. Eager passes with zero candidate adverse flags; its two >5% same-build p99 drift rows are listed below.

Ordinary planning allocation gates pass with observed candidate/baseline p50 ratios up to 1.000000000 for allocated bytes and 1.000061705 for incremental region peak live bytes; the allocation-analysis adverse list is empty. Allocation calls and reallocations remain diagnostics.

The refusal guard passes its explicit envelopes: candidate-invalid p50 is at most 1.102088711 times the baseline-valid p50 and candidate-invalid incremental peak is at most 1.000033688 times the baseline-valid peak. Valid p50 changes range from -24.945792% to -24.293636%. Those baseline-valid envelopes do not imply unchanged same-invalid latency or memory; the 32 normal and 222 allocation same-invalid flags are individually retained below.

All 10 cap size/repeat rows pass the candidate p50/mean <=1.05 matched-baseline limit; the largest observed p50/mean ratio is 1.026618698. Cap RSS changes range from -25.572960% to 2.796377%. The one cap repeat-drift row is retained separately. Main whole-child RSS changes range from -4.378647% to 4.592921%; RSS is diagnostic and is not converted into a latency claim.

Native phase evidence includes individually retained `open_ns`, `reopen_ns`, and `publication_ns` changes.
