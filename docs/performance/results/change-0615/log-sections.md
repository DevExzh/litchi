## HOTSPOTS.md

## 0615 — the execution context bounds one session at a time, and the workspace runs three

Design only, plus a proposed ADR for human review; no production change. Item CORE-3 of change 0587 (rank 35) named the gap from the source — `ExecutionLimits` has max workers, in-flight task and byte caps, `min_parallel_bytes`, affinity, cancellation and the hierarchical `Budget`, but no I/O concurrency limit, no CPU task budget distinct from byte-denominated `Work`, and no caller-provided executor — and this record measures it and finds the mechanism is not the pool duplication the survey described. `ExecutionLimits` is `Copy` and every session re-applies it; `Budget` is the only shared part of an `ExecutionContext`, and it has no dimension for workers, outstanding reads or task count, so `workers` is on the wrong side of the sharing boundary. A scratch probe drives all three parallel sessions — `soapberry_zip::office::ParallelReadSession`, `litchi_cfb::SharedOleBulkRead` and `litchi_opc::SourceBackedPackage::read_parts_ordered` — from one budget root with one policy saying `workers = W`, and counts, identically across five repeats at every width, **6, 12 and 20 session-owned worker threads at W = 2, 4 and 8** (`2W + min(W, tasks)`: two persistent Rayon pools plus one transient scoped wave), against a baseline of one; concurrent positional reads reach `min(W, independent work)` per session and 7–8 process-wide with two sources live. The pools do not leak — a 250 ms settle sample reports 1 at every width — but `ParallelReadSession` builds its pool in its constructor, so `W` threads exist before a byte is read, which is the one piece of this design that needs no ADR. Next in this area: gate G1 (make that pool lazy), then CORE-4 / SAVE-6 (parallel deflate of changed members), which is the most likely first real consumer — because at `1e4198321` every one of the three sessions has zero callers outside its own crate's tests, one example and the harness, and independent worksheets, slides, stories, sections, disjoint edit subplans, independent validation domains and output-member compression have no parallel path at all. `performance_claim: none`; no claim is registered. [Change and limitations](0615-execution-context-completeness-design.md); [proposed ADR 0031](../adr/0031-execution-context-budgets.md), not accepted; [retained evidence](results/change-0615/README.md).

## GOAL_AUDIT.md

## 0615 — the execution context bounds one session at a time, and the workspace runs three

`docs/GOAL.md` rule 9 requires CPU parallelism to be opt-in and controlled by an explicit execution context with thread, memory, I/O, cancellation and task-granularity budgets, and Workstream F names seven capabilities such a context must control. This record audits `litchi_core::ExecutionLimits` against that list and finds four and a half present: maximum worker count, the memory and decompression-in-flight budget, cancellation, and half of the task-size threshold — `min_parallel_bytes` is an aggregate with no per-task floor. Absent: I/O concurrency, a CPU task budget distinct from byte-denominated `Work`, and an optional caller-provided executor or scoped worker facility. Two further Workstream F requirements have no implementation at all: bounded overlapping pipelines (every session is wave-synchronous, and change 0499's contract is explicit that later waves cannot begin until the current one completes), and the dependency DAG itself — independent worksheets, slides, stories, sections, disjoint edit subplans, independent validation domains and compression of changed output members have no parallel path, and the three that do exist have no format-crate or facade caller. **No audit row closes.** The measured coexistence counts (6, 12 and 20 session-owned worker threads at `workers` 2, 4 and 8, from one budget root and one policy) are the evidence that the missing budgets must be hierarchical `Resource` dimensions rather than more per-session policy fields; the design is drafted as proposed ADR 0031, which amends the execution paragraph of accepted ADR 0005 and is therefore left for human review rather than implemented, exactly as `docs/GOAL.md` line 80 requires. No code changed, so lossless preservation, typed refusals and every limit are untouched. OLE2/OOXML optimization remains active; ODF is deferred until completion and iWork excluded. [Change and limitations](0615-execution-context-completeness-design.md); [retained evidence](results/change-0615/README.md).

## REPORT.md

## 0615 — three parallel sessions, one budget root, and 2W + min(W, tasks) threads

Design only plus a proposed ADR, `performance_claim: none`. No file under `crates/` changed and no timing was measured. A scratch probe drives the workspace's only three parallel sessions from one hierarchical `Budget` root with one `ExecutionLimits` saying `workers = W`, and counts live OS threads from `/proc/self/task`: baseline 1, then 0, 6, 12 and 20 session-owned worker threads at W = 1, 2, 4 and 8 — two persistent Rayon pools plus one transient scoped wave, the wave capped at the four available parts. Every one of those counts is identical across five repeats at every width. Concurrent positional reads, with a fixed 500 µs per-read instrument that models no production storage, reach `min(W, independent work)` per session (1, 2, 4, 4) and 7–8 process-wide with two sources live; the process-wide figure is a run-dependent lower bound, and one separately retained single run recorded 3 rather than 4 for the CFB session and is kept rather than discarded. Without the instrument the same probe reports per-session concurrency of 1, because a `read_at` over an in-memory `Vec` is a `memcpy` and four workers on one pinned core do not overlap; that leg is retained as the control which establishes the thread table, not the read table, as load-bearing. Nothing here got worse and nothing got better, because nothing ran twice: no A/B, no A/A, no allocation or RSS profile, no callgrind, no harness capture. Every scaling figure the record quotes — change 0009's 4.52× at 37.7% efficiency with a 15.0% serial fraction for OPC few-large, 5.93× at 49.4% and 9.3% for CFB, 0.73× and 0.52× for many-small; change 0498's 94.8 µs serial against 560.0 µs at four workers for sixty-four 16 KiB parts; change 0499's residual 186.04 µs against a 95.86 µs serial p50 — is **retained**, not re-measured, with its own out-of-model caveats reproduced. No speedup, regression, RSS, cold-cache, physical-device, range-source, scaling, contention, cross-platform or real-producer claim is registered. OLE2/OOXML remain active; ODF is deferred until completion and iWork excluded. [Change and limitations](0615-execution-context-completeness-design.md); [retained evidence](results/change-0615/README.md).

## ADR_COMPLIANCE.md

## 0615 — an ADR 0005 amendment, drafted rather than taken

ADR 0005 is the record this change would amend, and it is amended only as a **proposed** record: `docs/adr/0031-execution-context-budgets.md` is marked Proposed, awaiting human review, is deliberately absent from the accepted table in `docs/adr/README.md`, and states that no code may cite it until a human accepts it. `docs/GOAL.md` line 80 is the governing rule — an otherwise-desirable change that would conflict with an accepted record is recorded and, only when truly necessary, drafted as a separate proposed ADR — and that is what happened: nothing is implemented. ADR 0005's execution paragraph ("scheduling, affinity, cancellation, thread and memory budgets") would gain I/O concurrency, a CPU task budget and an optional caller-provided facility; its "production-safe desktop, server, and trusted-batch profiles are finite" clause would become a requirement on the three new dimensions, and its "there is no hidden global Rayon pool" clause is strengthened rather than weakened, since the design creates no path to one and gate G7 checks it. ADR 0002's dependency direction is preserved by construction: the new trait lives in `litchi-core`, which implements nothing; `soapberry-zip` — which does not depend on `litchi-core` and must not start to — mirrors it exactly as it already mirrors `CancellationProbe`, and `litchi-opc` bridges, exactly as it already bridges `ExecutionLimits` to `ParallelReadLimits`. `docs/GOAL.md` rule 7 is satisfied because the facility appears only on `ExecutionContext`, an advanced-ingress type no ordinary CRUD signature names, and acceptance is made conditional on an audit proving it. ADR 0003, 0006, 0010 and 0011 are untouched: no snapshot, edit, patch, validation, refusal or ownership boundary moves, and the one new refusal (`IoConcurrency` exhausted) is specified to fire before any source byte is read, which is stricter than any existing behaviour rather than weaker; rule 3 is what forces admission-time reservation over a mid-operation acquire. No file under `crates/` changed, so no `unsafe`, no limit, no malformed-input defence, no global pool, no ambient I/O and no public API moved at all. Gates: `cargo fmt --all --check` clean; `tools/check_perf_claims.py --mode strict` 10 claims validated; `tools/check_report_claim_classification.py` 167 rows across 2 tables; `tools/non_iwork_gate.py verify` 45 bulk tree roots, 35 facade safe trees and the combined facade tree verified; the retained probe's own `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy --release --all-targets` clean. `performance_claim: none`. See [Change 0615](0615-execution-context-completeness-design.md); [retained evidence](results/change-0615/README.md).
