Reading. No fixture names its producer: PIDSI_APPNAME (property 18) is
absent everywhere and poi/test.doc has no SummaryInformation stream at all.
The \x01CompObj user type 'Microsoft Word-Dokument' / 'Word.Document.8' is
what an exporter writes as readily as Word does, so it identifies nothing.
What does identify a producer is the stylesheet: 'WW-'-prefixed names are
the OpenOffice.org / LibreOffice ww8 filter's de-duplication of a style
name it could not keep (note duplicate-style-names.doc's
WW-Absatz-Standardschriftart, ...1, ...11, ...111 trail), and 'WW8Numn zn'
is that filter's list-level character styles. All five duplicate-style
fixtures and watermark.doc carry one or both; poi/test.doc carries neither
and is unattributed.
