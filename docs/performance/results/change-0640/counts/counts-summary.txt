Change 0640 - deterministic counts for the eager DOC open

Method: callgrind isolation pair per leg (profile 10 and 110 eager opens of
the same fixture, difference the `summary:` totals, divide by 100).
RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1, taskset -c 16, --cache-sim=no --branch-sim=no.
Binaries staged outside any Cargo target directory; see binaries.sha256.

fixture     end markers   before Ir/open    after Ir/open   delta Ir   delta %
utas                 67        4,153,320        4,150,518     -2,802  -0.0675%
floating              9        2,967,375        2,955,382    -11,992  -0.4041%

utas     = poi/test-data/document/au.edu.utas.www___data_assets_word_doc_0003_
           154335_International-Travel-Approval-Request-Form.doc (86.5 KB, 67
           field end markers, the most field-bearing fixture that opens)
floating = ole/doc/FloatingPictures.doc (9 field end markers)

The change removes two boolean comparisons per field end marker, i.e. at most
about 300 instructions on the 67-marker fixture. Both deltas above exceed that,
so they are code-layout drift rather than the removed work. An earlier pass of
this same pair, taken with a probe binary that differed only by one unused
extra subcommand, gave utas -1,518 Ir on the before leg alone and put the
after-minus-before delta at +339 Ir (+0.0082%) instead of -2,802 Ir: the same
litchi-doc change, measured through a differently laid-out driver, changes the
sign. The removed work is below this measurement's layout floor. No
instruction-count claim is made in either direction, and no timing was taken:
this change is a correctness fix, not an optimization.

Syscall census (strace -c, one eager open, before vs after): identical on both
fixtures. utas: 5 openat, 14 read, 14 lseek, 2 pread64, 5 close, 16 mmap.
FloatingPictures: 5 openat, 19 read, 19 lseek, 2 pread64, 5 close, 16 mmap.
